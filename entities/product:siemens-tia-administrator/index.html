    Entries about Siemens TIA Administrator (1)

    NOTABLECVE-2026-111497exploitedupdatedNATOA2

    CVE-2026-111497, Brotli decoder flaw in Siemens industrial and software products (Desigo CC, SIMATIC WinCC, Industrial Edge, Teamcenter and Opcenter among them), Mitel MiVoice Business and OpenScape, with Cisco listing Duo Network Gateway and RoomOS in its combined advisory: Desigo CC takes the .NET updates, while most Siemens products, the Mitel updates and the Industrial Edge Common Configurator fix are still pending

    The Brotli decoder flaw CVE-2026-111497 is an out-of-bounds write caused by improper handling of malformed compressed data (Mitel, 2026-10-28), and CISA lists it as exploited (CISA, 2026-10-27). Siemens' advisory SSA-045512, revised on 2026-11-06, lists Desigo CC V8 and V9, all versions, remedied by installing the latest .NET security updates via Windows Update, Gridscale X DSA from V5.3 below V5.4 (fixed in V5.4) and Gridscale X PSS®E below V36.8.0 (fixed in V36.8.0), and scores the flaw CVSS 3.1 9.8 (Siemens ProductCERT, 2026-10-30). Its Industrial Edge advisory SSA-443410 gives fixed versions for six configurator and monitoring applications, and for Common Configurator, which has no fix, says to uninstall all Management Applications and use a firewall or WAF, or stop the application on a device (Siemens ProductCERT, 2026-10-30). Two further Siemens advisories of 2026-11-10, both interim, list 15 industrial and 28 software products, with a fix only for WinCC OA V3.20 and BRAUMAT and SISTAR and mitigations that differ by product, some products having none; neither says anything about exploitation (Siemens ProductCERT, 2026-11-10) (Siemens ProductCERT, 2026-11-10).

    Mitel's MISA-2026-0010, revision 5.0 of 2026-11-04, rates the flaw high (CVSS 3.1 8.1) and lists MiVoice Business and five OpenScape products, up to the versions in its table, as affected; for each an update is planned, not released, and workaround articles exist for OpenScape SBC, SESAP and Contact Center (Mitel, 2026-10-28).

    Cisco's advisory, version 1.12 of 2026-11-12, says its PSIRT is aware that proof-of-concept exploit code is available and is not aware of any malicious use against any Cisco product; it lists Catalyst SD-WAN, Identity Services Engine, IOS XE, IOS XR and Secure Firewall ASA, FMC and FTD as not impacted (Cisco PSIRT, 2026-10-27). Its single fix table lists Duo Network Gateway (3.4.2), Spaces (24.22.1), RoomOS on Board, Desk and Room Series devices (on-premises 11.41.2.3 and 26.7.4.3) and a longer list whose fixes are dated November 2026 to March 2027 (Cisco PSIRT, 2026-10-27).

    Update12 Nov 2026 04:21 UTCrun 2026-11-12T0255Z-inteltitleheadlinesummaryaffected_productscvessourcessourcing_noteactionsbodyentities

    Siemens published SSA-242944 and SSA-678870 on 2026-11-10, each still interim, extending CVE-2026-111497 to 15 industrial and 28 software products (Siemens ProductCERT, 2026-11-10; Siemens ProductCERT, 2026-11-10). Fixes exist only for SIMATIC WinCC OA V3.20 below V3.20 P17.1 (update to V3.20 P17.1) and for BRAUMAT and SISTAR V8.2 Update 1 to below Update 5 (update to V8.2 Update 5); Siemens plans no fix for the SIMATIC Profiling Tool and lists none available for SIMATIC WinCC V7/V8, WinCC Unified, WinCC Runtime Professional, WinCC Companion, WinCC OA V3.21, TIA Administrator, the User Management Component, COMOS Walkinside, the Project Server Web Administration Tool or, in the software advisory, any of the 28 products, among them Teamcenter V2412 to V2606, the Opcenter family, PADS and Xpedition (Siemens ProductCERT, 2026-11-10; Siemens ProductCERT, 2026-11-10). For the industrial products the mitigation is to disable Brotli compression and decompression at the application or proxy layer where it is not required, and the User Management Component and, when UMC authentication is enabled, TIA Administrator also get an IIS request-filtering rule that denies the value br in the Content-Encoding header (Siemens ProductCERT, 2026-11-10). For the software products Siemens points Teamcenter, the Opcenter family and Process Preparation X to knowledge-base articles that need a Siemens login and were not readable without one, tells HyperLynx, PADS and Xpedition to disable Brotli compression and block it at the network firewall, and lists no mitigation for Innexis Architecture Native Acceleration, Insights Hub, PAVE360 Automotive and Simcenter Executable Digital Twin and Studio (Siemens ProductCERT, 2026-11-10). Polarion, Solid Edge, Calibre, Capital and four other software products are listed as not affected because Node.js is not present or predates Brotli (Siemens ProductCERT, 2026-11-10). Neither advisory says anything about exploitation; the exploited status rests on CISA's listing.
