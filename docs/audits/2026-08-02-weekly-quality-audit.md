## Verdict

**58 of 71 entries verified fully clean (81.7 %)**, against 56 % last window — and the improvement is real rather than a measurement artifact, because this window ran the stricter configuration: a 2:2 model split instead of 3:1 Opus, with the adjacency standard that produced last week's headline finding now applied exhaustively by all four passes. Zero broken URLs, zero wrong CVE identifiers, zero IOC leaks, and every ATT&CK id checked is active in the pinned dataset.

**The previous window's dominant defect class has materially receded.** Per-fact attribution — a true fact cited to a source that does not carry it — ran at 11 defects in 57 entries last window. It runs at 4 in 71 here, and the two Opus batches covering 40 operational entries found none at all; both surviving instances sit in the weekly strategic set. That is the v3.29 mechanisation working, and it repeats the v3.26 lesson exactly: the limb that was made mechanical moved, the limb left to judgement did not.

What replaced it is narrower and more mechanical still. **Quote fidelity is now the leading residual truth defect** — three instances in one week, including one sentence Unit 42 never wrote that also dropped 11 confirmed compromises from a campaign's impact count. Every one of the three would have been caught by a literal substring search against a page the composer had already fetched, which is what v3.30 now requires. Two further defects are the flip side of a rule this pipeline tightened deliberately: entries mapping an ATT&CK technique no source supports, in order to satisfy the mandatory-non-empty requirement on behaviour kinds. Completeness pressure without an evidence floor produces invention, so v3.30 supplies the floor.

Completeness was again the weaker half but by a smaller margin. Three genuine gaps were recovered and published. A fourth was confirmed, judged relevant, and **deliberately not published** because its per-CVE authority was unreachable on every transport — recorded here rather than quietly dropped, because an unpublishable gap the reader should know about is still a gap.

Machinery is healthy. No runaway runs, publish follow-through 10 of 10, the verifier rotation held perfectly in every fire, and both fixes the previous audit shipped for the source lifecycle are measurably working. One structural inefficiency was root-caused and fixed: a backup weekly spent 2.3 h and eight verification iterations before discovering, at the pre-push sync, that the primary had already published the week.
