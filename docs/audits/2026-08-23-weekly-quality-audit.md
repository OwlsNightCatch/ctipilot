## Findings — systemic / operational

**1. The run clock has been lying for three months — 101 of 153 records stamp `completed` before their own last verifier iteration.** Phase 5's telemetry step writes `main.ended_at` *before* the mechanical gate and the verifier loop, and nothing re-stamped it, so `duration_seconds` systematically excludes verification — typically one to two hours. Concretely: `2026-08-19T0410Z-intel` recorded 3 963 s while its iteration 7 ended at 07:18:13Z (true ≥ 11 269 s, **past the runaway threshold, which therefore never fired**) — and that run's own waiver prose said "passed the ~3 h guard at iteration 7 (186 min elapsed)": the words knew, the field lied. `2026-08-20T0409Z-intel` is the same shape. Both prior audits' "no runaway" telemetry verdicts were computed from these figures. Fixed in v3.32: the Phase 5 stamp is explicitly provisional, Phase 6 step 0 re-stamps at the true end, and `check_run.py` FAILs a v3.32+ record whose `completed` precedes any of its own sub-timestamps (`run-clock`); the 48 non-migrated legacy records are counted once, informationally, in `--all` and stay immutable. (Count provenance: this fire's first scan reported 98; its own verification loop's confirmation pass recounted with a stricter timestamp parser and found three more — the three 2026-07-14 fires, whose `ended_at` values the first parser silently failed on — settling at 101 of the 153 records in the Phase 0 store; the regenerated artifact `work/2026-08-23T1311Z-audit/completed-inversions.json` also includes the two weeklies that landed mid-audit, both showing the same pre-v3.32 inversion.)
