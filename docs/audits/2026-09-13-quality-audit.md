Here the thing that brought it back was a researcher publishing a bypass, which is luck rather than mechanism.

## Findings — systemic and operational

### 1. The verifier can be blocked by content, and the pipeline then has no verification at all

This is the window's headline finding and it is established by reproduction rather than by inference.

The **2026-09-09T1726Z-intel** fire published two entries with `verification.iterations: []`. Its record documents why in full, with request ids: the pinned Sonnet `cti-verification` definition was terminated by the content-safety classifier (`[cyber]`) on all four attempts of the retry ladder — the initial spawn, a defensive-role reframe, a minimal pointer-only message, and a fourth carrying no offensive vocabulary at all with scope handed via a manifest file. The fire published under the documented Phase 5.7 fail-open and asked, in its own notes, for the next quality audit to give those two entries an independent pass.

This audit attempted exactly that, and reproduced the trip three more times:

| Attempt | Scope | Framing | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| Batch C | 15 entries incl. both | Standard audit truth-pass envelope | `[cyber]`, killed **mid-flight** after real work |
| Batch C1 | 7 entries incl. both | Defensive fact-checker framing, scope via manifest file | `[cyber]`, killed **on spawn** |
| Batch C1a | **2 entries** — the pair alone | Narrowest possible scope, per-entry checkpointing, no offensive vocabulary | `[cyber]`, killed **on spawn** |

Splitting the same 15 entries the other way worked first time: batch C1b (5 entries) and batch C2 (8 entries) both completed normally and returned substantive results. **Seven blocked spawns across two fires and four distinct framings, against content that two sibling batches processed without incident, establishes the trip as a property of this content rather than of the message.** Reframing was not going to work on the eighth attempt.

The consequence is the part that matters. The main agent verified the two entries itself and **found a factual error in one of them** — the Patch Tuesday count above — while confirming everything else the fire had claimed, including both MSRC quotes verbatim, both CVSS scores, both exploitation flags, both KEV dates and the ZDI quote. So the missing pass was not a formality: it was the pass that would have caught the only defect in the entry.

**What shipped (v4.10):**

- `prompts/cti-run.md` § Phase 5.7 gains an **exhausted-ladder rung**. When every rung fails, the fail-open still stands — guard #1 is absolute, a missing run record is worse than a missing verifier — but it is no longer a licence to publish unchecked. The main agent must now take the **truth half** of the gate on its own output before committing: re-read each to-be-published entry against its cited primaries, `grep -F` every quote against the saved body, re-check every CVE id, CVSS and affected/fixed pair against the per-CVE authority, confirm every ATT&CK id is active in the pin — and record exactly that, naming what it did and what it could not do. The rung states the limit plainly: the main agent substitutes for the truth gate, never for the independent editorial cold read, because it is checking its own composition.
- The § Hard rules "at least one iteration is mandatory" line now names its single exception and fences it, so it cannot be read as a general escape: a spawn that returned late, timed out, or was never attempted is **not** an exhausted ladder.
- `tools/check_run.py` carries **store severity** for `verification.iterations missing or empty` under `--all` only. This was a genuine dead end: run records are immutable, and `fail()` — unlike `warn()` — never consults the acknowledgment ledger, so the FAIL could not be cleared by any fix or any reviewed decision, and `--all` was permanently red for a fire that followed the prompt exactly. **In run scope it remains a FAIL**, verified against the 2026-09-09 record. This is deliberately not the self-serve mute button the ledger has refused five times over: nothing auto-passes on a non-null `confirmation_waived`, and the downgrade keys on the scan being retrospective, not on the run's own prose.

**What did not ship, and why.** No model override. The obvious move — spawn the verifier on Opus when Sonnet trips — is available and was not taken. The pin exists so the verifier never follows the main agent, and on a fire where the main agent is Opus, an Opus verifier confirming that main agent's own composition is precisely the independence the new rung is written to disclaim. Trading the gate's independence for its availability makes the record look better and the verification worse.
