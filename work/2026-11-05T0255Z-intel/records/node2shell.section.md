Splunk's advisory SVD-2026-1015, published on 2026-10-28 and updated on 2026-11-04, lists Splunk Enterprise, the Splunk Visual Exporter app, SOAR Automation Broker, UBA, Attack Analyzer, Python for Scientific Computing, several AppDynamics components and Smartlook as including packages affected by four Node.js flaws, CVE-2026-111497 among them; the page gives no CVSS score, no attacker position and no exploitation statement, and it covers the Brotli flaw and three DNS and TLS hostname-validation flaws together, so it does not show which of them reaches a given product ([Splunk, 2026-11-04](https://advisory.splunk.com/advisories/SVD-2026-1015)). Splunk Enterprise 10.0 and lower is vulnerable, Splunk recommends upgrading to 10.2 or higher for the bundled Node.js runtime and plans to upgrade the packages in a future 10.0 release, and the upgrade does not remediate the Visual Exporter app, which every Splunk Enterprise version includes and which Splunk says to disable or remove ([Splunk, 2026-11-04](https://advisory.splunk.com/advisories/SVD-2026-1015)). The page gives no fix for the other listed products.

Citrix bulletin CTX697300 lists Unicon eLux Scout as affected by CVE-2026-111497 alone and names the fixes as hotfix 2609.1 for Scout On-Prem, 2508 CU2 LTSR for Scout On-Prem LTSR and 2605.1-3 for Scout Cloud Gateway On-Prem, without a vulnerable version range or an exploitation statement ([Citrix, 2026-11-03](https://support.citrix.com/external/article/CTX697300/citrix-security-advisory-for-cve-2026-11.html)). Neither Splunk nor Citrix says anything on malicious use against its products.
