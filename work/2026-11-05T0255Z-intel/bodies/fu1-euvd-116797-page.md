### Summary

SEPPmail Secure Email Gateway before 15.1.1 does not neutralize LDAP metacharacters in an unauthenticated account-selection input in its GINA web interface. A remote unauthenticated attacker can enumerate email addresses.

### Affected Product

| Vendor: | Product: | Version: |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SEPPmail AG | SEPPmail Secure Email Gateway (SEG) | 0 <15.1.1 |
