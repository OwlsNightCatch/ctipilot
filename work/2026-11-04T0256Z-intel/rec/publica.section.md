The Pensionskasse Stadt Zürich's foundation board suspended the introduction of PK Softech's administration system planned for early 2027 because of the supplier's cyberattack and will keep operating its existing system in 2027; it will decide whether, when and under what conditions to introduce a new system only once the attack and the possible data loss are worked through (translated from German) ([Inside IT, 2026-11-03](https://www.inside-it.ch/pensionskasse-zuerich-sistiert-softwaremigration-20261103)). PK Softech, quoted by watson.ch, says it cannot rule out that non-pseudonymised data was temporarily present in project-related work, and the Federal Prosecutor's Office confirmed to watson.ch that a notorious extortion gang is behind the attack and has added the case to its criminal proceeding against the Akira group, open since April 2024 (translated from German) ([watson.ch, 2026-11-03](https://www.watson.ch/schweiz/digital/705538861-pk-softech-ag-und-publica-ransomware-attacke-wirft-neue-fragen-auf)).
