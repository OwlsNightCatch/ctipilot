Vaud's cantonal police announced on 2026-11-03 that a 16-year-old was arrested on Saturday 2026-10-31, suspected of intruding into the IT-support platform of the canton's post-compulsory schools and extracting data from it between 23 and 26 October 2026 (translated from French) ([Le Temps, 2026-11-03](https://www.letemps.ch/suisse/vaud/cyberattaque-contre-les-ecoles-vaudoises-un-adolescent-de-16-ans-a-ete-interpelle)). The education directorate says the suspect is one of its pupils and faces an administrative sanction, up to exclusion, that runs apart from the criminal case; he was questioned and released, his IT equipment was seized and is being analysed, and the investigation, led by the juvenile court, aims to establish the circumstances of the intrusion and whether data were downloaded or passed to third parties ([Le Temps, 2026-11-03](https://www.letemps.ch/suisse/vaud/cyberattaque-contre-les-ecoles-vaudoises-un-adolescent-de-16-ans-a-ete-interpelle); [Inside IT, 2026-11-03](https://www.inside-it.ch/16-jaehriger-hackte-wohl-waadtlaender-schulen-20261103)). The police add that the platform was disconnected from the internet on detection and then secured with the support of the canton's security operations centre; the access method and the platform product are still not stated.
