Ivanti's own CVE record for CVE-2026-118527, published on 2026-11-03, describes predictable filenames in Connect Secure before 25.1.1.3 and 22.8R2.6 that let a remote authenticated attacker upload files with attacker-controlled content to a known location on disk ([ENISA EUVD, 2026-11-03](https://euvd.enisa.europa.eu/vulnerability/EUVD-2026-115872)). CERT-FR's advisory of 2026-11-02 relays Ivanti's statement that CVE-2026-104935, CVE-2026-111357 and CVE-2026-118527 are all actively exploited, lists Connect Secure 22.8Rx before 22.8R2.6, 25.1.1.x before 25.1.1.3 and 25.1.2.x before 25.1.3.1 as affected, and says Ivanti states that the 25.1.3.x releases are not affected by the last two (translated from French) ([CERT-FR, 2026-11-02](https://www.cert.cyber.gouv.fr/avis/CERTFR-2026-AVI-1442/)).
