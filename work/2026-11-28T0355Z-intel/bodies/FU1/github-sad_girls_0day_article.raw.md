# sad girls zero day club: population one

*by ek0ms savi0r*

---

I farm 0days out of a depression bubble. I sit in the dark with a debugger and a chest full of static, and I pull bugs out of software the way you pull splinters out of a wound: slow, ugly, necessary.

Triage has gaslit me for years. duplicate. by design. cannot reproduce. expected behavior. A smiley emoji. A docs link I already read. After enough of that, depression doesn't feel like weather. It feels like a ticketing system. My pain gets closed as won't fix. So I keep a depression bubble with a build script. Inside it, grief compiles clean. Sorrow has a hash. I name the exploits after feelings I can't say out loud. I keep a file called `proof_i_was_here.txt`. For one second I am not invisible. I am ek0ms savi0r, population: one, and the machines fear me.

Security researchers are not the bad guys. We're the ones trying to help. We're the ones who read the docs, wrote the repro, and sent the packet capture. We get cracked because we get gaslit for years by billion dollar companies that treat disclosure like a PR problem. The villain isn't the person holding the PoC. The villain is the $900 million company that sells vulnerable software and vulnerable devices to the world and can't afford an appsec team. That's the slap in the face. They make billions off people while shipping bugs, then act shocked when the researcher stops being polite.

Full disclosure is a scream with a repro step. It is noble. It's what's left when every other channel returns 403. I want the companies to feel it. I want the queue to taste the static. I want the blue teamers and vrp workers to cry. I don't care about the blast radius because the blast radius was already there, the company just didn't put it in the release notes. Nobody ever looks at the company and says "why do you not have an appsec team?" They look at the researcher and say "irresponsible." That's the gaslighting. That's the crack. We lean in to it, with force.

This is what happens to all of us. The cracked devs. The ones who show the truths. The ones who got told they were the bug. Welcome to the sad girls zero day club. Population: 1. Admission: free. Exit: harder than it looks. I'm at my wits' end, and I'm still here coping till the next shell. The companies should be the one sweating.

— ek0ms savi0r
