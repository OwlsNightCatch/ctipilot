## Key facts

For around 20 years, the civil Federal Administration has been using SAP’s standard software for its support processes (finance, personnel, logistics and procurement, and real estate). In 2015, SAP announced that it was completely overhauling this software and that the previous version would be neither further developed nor supported from the end of 2025. It is thus imperative that it be replaced. In 2017, the Federal Council decided to switch to the new SAP solution (S/4HANA). It launched two implementation programmes for this purpose: SUPERB for the civil Federal Administration, ERP systems D/ar for the operationally relevant administrative units (Defence, etc.).
