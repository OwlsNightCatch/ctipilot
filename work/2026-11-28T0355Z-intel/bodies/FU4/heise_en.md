# SAP IT incident affects development systems

SAP has acknowledged an IT incident. Development systems on the GitHub platform are reportedly affected.

Details remain scarce, but German software company [SAP](https://heise.de/thema/SAP) has confirmed an IT incident to several media outlets. The company reportedly detected suspicious activity. In response, SAP “immediately took the necessary steps to protect its customers and ensure the security of its services,” the company told the German Press Agency (dpa), among others.

A few days ago, SAP had already informed employees about the suspicious activity in a letter. Technicians reportedly detected it on the “GitHub platform for developer productivity and software delivery”. It remains unclear which repositories and projects were affected. The letter apparently asks employees to check whether they need to take security measures and, for example, change their login credentials. Citing sources within the company, [Handelsblatt](https://www.handelsblatt.com/technik/ki/sap-it-sicherheitsvorfall-haben-verdaechtige-aktivitaeten-identifiziert/100273138.html) reports that SAP restricted access to GitHub, halted automated software development processes and replaced signing keys. GitHub has been a major focus of cybercriminals in recent months. They repeatedly manage to [hijack thousands of GitHub repositories and abuse that access](https://heise.de/news/Gestohlene-Token-Secret-Stealer-in-Tausenden-GitHub-Repositories-11524706.html?from-en=1), most recently drawing attention in mid-November.

[Spiegel](https://www.spiegel.de/netzwelt/sap-wird-opfer-von-hackerangriff-a-6648b5ce-5d74-48dc-86ea-9469705bb91d) describes a “cyberattack”, which the company reportedly also confirmed. SAP initially did not confirm that to dpa, however. SAP has not disclosed the nature of the suspicious activity, exactly when it was detected or which areas were affected. According to the newsmagazine, SAP internally refers to the incident by the codename “Project Granite.” Sources within the company say the attackers may have used agentic AI. However, based on the current state of the investigation, there are no indications of unauthorized access to customers’ business data; the DAX-listed company adds the usual assurances about [cyberattacks](https://heise.de/thema/Cyberangriff), saying it continuously improves its security measures.

### More high-profile cyberattack victims

Recently, a growing number of high-profile organizations have been hit by IT incidents. Along with U.S. agencies such as a [financial crime unit](https://heise.de/news/Cyberangriff-auf-US-Behoerde-gegen-Finanzkriminalitaet-11539155.html?from-en=1), the [CIA](https://heise.de/news/Cyberangriff-auf-US-Geheimdienst-CIA-11537061.html?from-en=1) and the [FBI](https://heise.de/news/Medienbericht-Zentrale-FBI-Datenbank-angegriffen-11533560.html?from-en=1), as well as the [international soccer federation FIFA](https://heise.de/news/Warnung-vor-erheblichen-Folgen-Erfolgreicher-Cyberangriff-auf-die-FIFA-11539255.html?from-en=1), German companies [such as Deichmann](https://heise.de/news/Deichmann-Online-Shop-geknackt-unbefugter-Datenzugriff-11537484.html?from-en=1) and [Hugendubel](https://heise.de/news/Nach-Cyberangriff-Hugendubel-Tolino-Cloud-und-eBook-de-gestoert-11534213.html?from-en=1) have also recently been targeted by cyberattacks.
