## Overview

- [SAP](https://particle.news/entity/sap/m/01pf4l?ref=cluster:21955908) detected suspicious activity on its [GitHub](https://particle.news/entity/github/m/0ryppmg?ref=cluster:21955908) developer and delivery platform and alerted staff internally before making public statements.
- The company told media it took immediate protective measures to safeguard services and customers and said its probe so far shows no signs of customer business-data access.
-  Reporting based on internal sources says the incident is codenamed “Project Granite” inside [SAP](https://particle.news/entity/sap/m/01pf4l?ref=cluster:21955908) and caused significant internal alarm over several days.
- Unconfirmed technical claims from insiders allege attackers stole employee authentication tokens and copied or manipulated repositories, with some reports suggesting the use of agentic (autonomous) AI to automate the intrusion.
-  Corporate sources report measures taken include restricting [GitHub](https://particle.news/entity/github/m/0ryppmg?ref=cluster:21955908) access, pausing automated CI/CD workflows, and rotating signing keys, while investigators and [SAP](https://particle.news/entity/sap/m/01pf4l?ref=cluster:21955908) continue to assess scope and impact and regulators warn agentic AI could lower the bar for complex attacks.
