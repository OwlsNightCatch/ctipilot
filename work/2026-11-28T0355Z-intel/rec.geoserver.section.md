CISA added CVE-2026-120705, the unauthenticated XML injection in the WFS GetFeatureById stored query, and CVE-2026-129243, the service-level authorization bypass through a prefixed service name, to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog on 2026-11-27, saying it did so on evidence of active exploitation ([CISA, 2026-11-27](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/alerts/2026/11/27/cisa-adds-three-known-exploited-vulnerabilities-catalog)). CISA names no actor, victim, scale or source for that evidence, so whether it saw watchTowr's activity or its own cannot be told. The alert lists only those two GeoServer flaws; watchTowr's FAQ table marks CVE-2026-129245 as exploited as well but not CVE-2026-129244 or CVE-2026-129242 ([watchTowr, 2026-11-25](https://watchtowr.com/intelligence/geoserver-cve-2026-129245-faq/)), and no source marks CVE-2026-129242, the administrator-only deserialization step, as exploited, so its exploited status is removed from the record.
