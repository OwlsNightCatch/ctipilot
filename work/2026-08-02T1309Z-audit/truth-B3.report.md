**Claim (frontmatter `summary`, verbatim):**
> "Minnesota IT Services announced on 2026-07-28 that more than 30 communities had water and wastewater utilities disrupted by a coordinated cyberattack over 26–27 July, **affecting programmable logic controllers and cellular-connected equipment at water towers and lift stations**."

**Ground truth (StateScoop, 2026-07-28, fetched this pass):** no cited source places PLCs in the Minnesota event. The summary is the machine-consumed field a triage agent reads first, and it converts an explicitly-open question into a stated fact — the single thing this otherwise exemplary entry works hardest to hold open. The cellular half of the clause is sound.

**Everything else on this entry verified correct**, including both AA26-097A evidence quotes (verbatim), the "leased, third-party hosted infrastructure" + Studio 5000 / EcoStruxure Control Expert / TIA Portal chain, the Dropbear-SSH-on-modem instance, CISA's "not highlighting a new vulnerability in the identified products, but instead discusses opportunistic targeting" statement, the run-position mitigation behind action 2, and the deliberate non-attribution boundary (StateScoop's "though Iran is a reasonable guess" is correctly identified as the reporter's own inference and is not carried).
