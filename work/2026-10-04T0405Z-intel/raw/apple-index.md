# Apple security releases

This document lists security updates for Apple software.

## About Apple security releases

For the protection of our customers, Apple doesn't disclose, discuss, or confirm security issues until an investigation has occurred and patches or releases are generally available. This document lists recent releases, including software updates and [Background Security Improvements](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102657) that are accompanied by security advisories with relevant [CVE-ID](https://www.cve.org) details.

If you need technical support for a security issue—for example, to reset your Apple Account password or to review a recent App Store charge—view the [Get help with security issues](https://support.apple.com/en-us/111756) article.

If you believe that you've discovered a security or privacy vulnerability in an Apple product, learn how to [submit your research](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102549).

## Get the latest software updates from Apple

Keeping your software up to date is one of the most important things you can do to maintain your Apple product's security.

- The latest version of iOS and iPadOS is 27.0.1. Learn how to [update the software on your iPhone or iPad](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118575) .
- The latest version of macOS is 27.0.1. Learn how to [update the software on your Mac](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108382) and how to allow important[background updates](https://support.apple.com/en-us/101591) .
- The latest version of tvOS is 27. Learn how to [update the software on your Apple TV](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108414) .
- The latest version of watchOS is 27.0.1. Learn how to [update the software on your Apple Watch](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108926) .
- The latest version of visionOS is 27.0.1. Learn how to [update the software on your Apple Vision Pro](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118481) .

Note that after a software update is installed for iOS, iPadOS, tvOS, watchOS, and visionOS, it cannot be downgraded to the previous version.
