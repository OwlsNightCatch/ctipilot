SANS Internet Storm Center's Cowrie SSH honeypots independently record the attempts: ISC dates the first genuine sshole attempt to 2026-11-05, with an all-"A" password, and counts long-password attempts at 5 on 2026-11-03, 58 on 11-04, 232 on 11-05, 713 on 11-06, 246 on 11-07 and 51 on 11-08, from sources spread across commercial hosting and telecom networks ([SANS ISC, 2026-11-08](https://isc.sans.edu/diary/33498)). The honeypots keep only the first 255 characters of a password, so the counts show attempts, not success, although the author expects that several of the logins succeeded on its honeypots and promises a follow-up ([SANS ISC, 2026-11-08](https://isc.sans.edu/diary/33498)). The 23 long passwords of 2026-11-01 predate the disclosure and, per ISC, are buggy brute-force scripts that typed shell commands into the password field ([SANS ISC, 2026-11-08](https://isc.sans.edu/diary/33498)). Two sensor sets, ISC's from 2026-11-05 and Previdian's from 2026-11-06, now record exploitation attempts; neither source nor any authority reports a compromised production host.
