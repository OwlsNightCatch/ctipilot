Code execution is not limited to hosts with ASLR disabled. depthfirst's proof of concept runs with ASLR off, but depthfirst notes that forked NGINX workers share one heap layout, which could in theory be used to leak ASLR ([depthfirst, 2026-05-13](https://depthfirst.com/research/nginx-rift-achieving-nginx-rce-via-an-18-year-old-vulnerability)), and F5's CVE description, updated on 2026-09-10, covers systems with ASLR disabled or where the attacker can bypass ASLR ([ENISA EUVD, 2026-09-10](https://euvd.enisa.europa.eu/vulnerability/EUVD-2026-30010)). An enabled ASLR is therefore no reason to defer the upgrade.
