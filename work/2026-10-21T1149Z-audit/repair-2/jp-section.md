The Digital Agency's own notice of 2026-09-11 confirms the 25 June detection and the 9 July finding that a third party had entered through a VPN vulnerability ([Digital Agency, 2026-09-11](https://www.digital.go.jp/news/2026-0911-01)). It splits the roughly 246,000 records into about 189,000 on staff of the agencies using GSS and the public servants working on their business (staff of independent administrative agencies included) and about 57,000 on the businesses and individuals involved, and besides reviewing vulnerability management it commits to improving how connections from outside are made ([Digital Agency, 2026-09-11](https://www.digital.go.jp/news/2026-0911-01)).
