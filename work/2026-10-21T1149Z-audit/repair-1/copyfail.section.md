Blacklisting `algif_aead` does not work on Red Hat Enterprise Linux: Red Hat states that the module "cannot be blocklisted due to being built into the kernel" and gives the boot argument `initcall_blacklist=algif_aead_init` to block the affected functions, with `initcall_blacklist=af_alg_init` as an alternative that blocks the AF_ALG interface itself; it warns of possible performance impact on functionality that uses kernel cryptographic functions, and states that all its fixes are available ([Red Hat RHSB-2026-002](https://access.redhat.com/security/vulnerabilities/RHSB-2026-002)). Xint also gives blocking AF_ALG socket creation with seccomp as an interim measure ([Xint, 2026-04-29](https://xint.io/blog/copy-fail-linux-distributions)).
