Cisco's indicators cover three CLI upload scripts in `/var/log/scripts.log`, `vconfd_script_upload_tenant_list.sh`, `vconfd_script_upload_vsmart_serial_numbers.sh` and `vconfd_script_upload_chassis_number_file.sh`, not only the tenant-list one; Cisco has also seen limited cases where exploitation pushed a configuration change to edge devices, asks customers to verify the configuration of the edge devices, and describes its Live Protect shield as only temporary partial protection ([Cisco PSIRT](https://sec.cloudapps.cisco.com/security/center/content/CiscoSecurityAdvisory/cisco-sa-sdwan-privesc-4uxFrdzx)). Mandiant's hunting guidance covers `vmanage-admin` logins from unexpected sources, `admin` password changes set and then reverted, and `su` from `admin` to unauthorized accounts in `/var/log/auth.log`, password delta commits under `/var/confd/rollback/`, and the upload command in the CLI's `show history`; its actor restored the system configurations it modified and checked that its root account was gone ([Mandiant, 2026-06-24](https://cloud.google.com/blog/topics/threat-intelligence/zero-day-exploitation-cisco-catalyst-sd-wan-manager)).
