## Item 1: EU Digital Omnibus AI Act — High-Risk Deadline Extension (NEW)

**Publication date:** 2026-05-07 (Council/Parliament agreement), 2026-05-08 (press coverage)

The EU Parliament and Council reached provisional agreement on 7 May 2026 under the Digital Omnibus package to amend the AI Act. Key points for defenders:

- **Annex III high-risk AI systems** (biometrics, critical infrastructure, education, employment, law enforcement, border management) — compliance deadline extended from 2 August 2026 to **2 December 2027** (+16 months).
- **Annex I product-embedded high-risk AI** (medical devices, machinery, etc.) — extended to **2 August 2028**.
- **Cybersecurity obligations** under Articles 8–15 still apply from 2 December 2027: robustness against adversarial attacks (prompt injection, data poisoning, model extraction), mandatory logging, CE marking, EU AI database registration.
- **New prohibition** on AI generating non-consensual sexual content (incl. CSAM) — effective 2 December 2026.
- AI Office competence boundaries for GPAI model supervision clarified vs. national authorities.
- Rationale: harmonised technical standards and Commission guidance needed for conformity assessments do not exist in final form.

**Defender relevance:** Public-sector entities deploying AI for recruitment screening, benefits assessment, risk scoring, or law-enforcement analytics must update compliance roadmaps to the 2 December 2027 deadline. GPAI-backed agentic AI systems not qualifying as high-risk under Annex III still face the existing GPAI-layer obligations.

**Sources:** TechPolicy.Press (2026-05-08), Lexology/Stephenson Harwood (2026-05-08)

## Item 3: CRA Implementation Milestones Approaching (NEW)

Two near-term CRA milestones:
- **11 June 2026** (25 days away): Conformity Assessment Body notification provisions apply. Member states must have designated notifying authorities.
- **11 September 2026** (117 days away): Article 14 reporting obligations apply — manufacturers must report actively exploited vulnerabilities and severe security incidents to national CSIRTs within 24 hours (initial notification), 72 hours (detailed report).

Background: Delegated Regulation (EU) 2026/881 on delayed dissemination of sensitive notifications was published 20 April 2026. Full CRA application: 11 December 2027.

**Defender relevance:** ICT procurement teams should ensure new connected-product contracts include CRA Article 14 pass-through obligations. CSIRT teams should prepare for increased inbound vulnerability notifications from product manufacturers.

**Source:** European Commission CRA Implementation fact page, https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/factpages/cyber-resilience-act-implementation
