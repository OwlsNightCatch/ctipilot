**#1 (F4).** `cve-2026-86206-86207-86218-n-able-n-central-third-chain.md` — the `cves[]` record for CVE-2026-86207 states `auth: pre-auth`. I fetched the entry's own cited CNA authority, OffSeq Threat Radar's per-CVE record (`https://radar.offseq.com/threat/cve-2026-86207-cwe-305-authentication-bypass-by-primary-weakness-in-n-able-n-central-fdc4e7f222848fbd`), via raw JSON embedded in the page: `"cveId":"CVE-2026-86207",...,"cvssScore":7.7,"cvssVector":"CVSS:4.0/AV:N/AC:L/AT:P/PR:L/UI:N/VC:H/VI:H/VA:H/SC:N/SI:N/SA:N","severity":"high"`. Under CVSS v4.0, `PR:L` ("Privileges Required: Low") means the attacker needs some pre-existing low-level privileges before exploiting, and `AT:P` ("Attack Requirements: Present") means the attack depends on conditions beyond the attacker's control — the opposite of a zero-privilege "pre-auth" claim. By contrast, I confirmed CVE-2026-86206 (`"cvssVector":"CVSS:4.0/AV:N/AC:L/AT:N/PR:N/...`) and CVE-2026-86218 (`"cvssVector":"CVSS:4.0/AV:N/AC:L/AT:N/PR:N/...`) both genuinely carry `PR:N`/`AT:N` (true pre-auth), so the entry's `auth: pre-auth` is correct for those two but specifically wrong for CVE-2026-86207 per its own cited CNA record (N-able is also the CVE-2026-86207 assigner, so this is an inconsistency within N-able's own data, not a third-party error). The entry's sourcing_note already discusses a vendor-blog-vs-CNA-record discrepancy for the CVSS *score* ("N-able's own HF3 blog post describes both CVE-2026-86206 and CVE-2026-86207 jointly as 'high-CVSS-rated'... this entry follows the per-CVE score") but never surfaces this second, separate discrepancy on the *auth/privilege* dimension. Fix: either correct CVE-2026-86207's `auth` field to reflect the OffSeq vector (e.g. `low-priv` or equivalent) or add an explicit sourcing_note clause disclosing that the CNA's own CVSS v4.0 vector for CVE-2026-86207 encodes `PR:L`/`AT:P`, unlike N-able's blog framing and unlike its two sibling CVEs.
