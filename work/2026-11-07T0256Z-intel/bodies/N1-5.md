## Critical SonicWall SonicOS vulnerabilities: [#](https://www.runzero.com#critical-sonicWall-sonicOS-vulnerabilities)

SonicWall [disclosed](https://psirt.global.sonicwall.com/vuln-detail/SNWLID-2026-0014) that certain versions of SonicOS on Gen7 and Gen8 firewalls are affected by multiple vulnerabilities:

- [CVE-2026-107155](https://www.cve.org/cverecord?id=CVE-2026-107155): A permissive cross-domain policy vulnerability in the SonicOS management interface could, under specific conditions, allow a remote, unauthenticated attacker to perform unauthorized operations. The vulnerability has been designated **CVE-2026-107155** and has been rated *high* with a CVSS score of **8.8**.
- [CVE-2026-107154](https://www.cve.org/cverecord?id=CVE-2026-107154): A session validation vulnerability in the SonicOS management interface could, under specific conditions, allow a remote, unauthenticated attacker to perform unauthorized operations. The vulnerability has been designated **CVE-2026-107154** and has been rated *high* with a CVSS score of **8.8**.
- [CVE-2026-82308](https://www.cve.org/cverecord?id=CVE-2026-82308): A pre-authentication heap-based buffer overflow in the SonicOS SSL-VPN service could allow a remote, unauthenticated attacker to cause an application-level denial of service. The vulnerability has been designated **CVE-2026-82308** and has been rated *high* with a CVSS score of **7.5**.
- [CVE-2026-19868](https://www.cve.org/cverecord?id=CVE-2026-19868): An authentication enforcement vulnerability in the SonicOS SSLVPN service may allow a user with valid credentials for an alternate authentication method to bypass SAML authentication controls, including SAML-enforced multi-factor authentication (MFA). This vulnerability only affects SSLVPN when SAML is enabled for SSLVPN logins. The vulnerability has been designated **CVE-2026-19868** and has been rated *high* with a CVSS score of **7.5**.
- [CVE-2026-77708](https://www.cve.org/cverecord?id=CVE-2026-77708): A pre-authentication stack-based buffer overflow in the SonicOS TACACS+ authentication client could, under specific conditions, allow a remote, unauthenticated attacker to cause an application-level denial of service.The vulnerability has been designated **CVE-2026-77708** and has been rated *medium* with a CVSS score of **5.9**.
- [CVE-2026-77709](https://www.cve.org/cverecord?id=CVE-2026-77709): A pre-authentication heap-based buffer overflow in the SonicOS TACACS+ authentication client could, under specific conditions, allow a remote, unauthenticated attacker to cause an application-level denial of service. The vulnerability has been designated **CVE-2026-77709** and has been rated *medium* with a CVSS score of **5.9**.
- [CVE-2026-19703](https://www.cve.org/cverecord?id=CVE-2026-19703): A weak password hashing vulnerability on SonicOS virtual appliances (NSv) could, under specific conditions, allow an attacker with existing local access to disclose a platform credential that provides access to a limited subset of platform settings. The vulnerability has been designated **CVE-2026-19868** and has been rated *medium* with a CVSS score of **5.1**.

[Evidence](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/alerts/2026/11/06/cisa-adds-one-known-exploited-vulnerability-catalog) indicates that **CVE-2026-19868 is actively exploited in the wild**, leading to its addition to the CISA Known Exploited Vulnerabilities (KEV) catalog on November 6, 2026.

**The following versions are affected**

- SonicWall Gen7 Firewalls (TZ 270, TZ 270W, TZ 370, TZ 370W, TZ 470, TZ 470W, TZ 570, TZ 570W, TZ 570P, TZ 670, NSa 2700, NSa 3700, NSa 4700, NSa 5700, NSa 6700, NSsp 10700, NSsp 11700, NSsp 13700, NSsp 15700) and Gen7 NSv (NSv 270, NSv 470, NSv 870 on ESX, KVM, Hyper-V, AWS, and Azure): SonicOS 7.3.3-7015 and earlier.
- SonicWall Gen8 Firewalls (TZ 80, TZ 280, TZ 280W, TZ 380, TZ 380W, TZ 480, TZ 580, TZ 680, NSa 2800, NSa 3800, NSa 4800, NSa 5800) and Gen8 NSv (NSv XS, NSv S, NSv M, NSv L on ESX, KVM, Hyper-V, AWS, and Azure): SonicOS 8.2.2-8015 and earlier.

## What is SonicWall SonicOS? [#](https://www.runzero.com#what-is-sonicWall-sonicOS)

SonicWall SonicOS is the operating system that manages the networking, routing, and security functions of SonicWall physical and virtual firewall appliances.

## What is the impact? [#](https://www.runzero.com#what-is-the-impact)

Successful exploitation of these vulnerabilities would allow adversaries to compromise firewall integrity, circumvent enterprise authentication controls to gain unauthorized network access, and cause service disruptions across critical perimeter infrastructure.

## Are updates or workarounds available? [#](https://www.runzero.com#are-updates-or-workarounds-available)

Users are encouraged to update to the latest version as quickly as possible:

- SonicWall Gen7 Firewalls and Gen7 NSv: Upgrade to SonicOS 7.3.4-7013 or later.
- SonicWall Gen8 Firewalls and Gen8 NSv: Upgrade to SonicOS 8.2.3-8015 or later.

SonicWall has published no workaround for these vulnerabilities.

## October 2026: CVE-2026-102255, CVE-2026-102256, CVE-2026-102257, and CVE-2026-102258 [#](https://www.runzero.com#october-2026-cve-2026-102255-cve-2026-102256-cve-2026-102257-and-cve-2026-102258)

SonicWall [disclosed](https://psirt.global.sonicwall.com/vuln-detail/SNWLID-2026-0017) that certain versions of SonicWall SMA1000 appliances are affected by multiple vulnerabilities:

- [CVE-2026-102255](https://www.cve.org/cverecord?id=CVE-2026-102255): A pre-authentication server-side request forgery (SSRF) vulnerability in the SMA1000 Work Place interface, caused by an unintended alternate access path (forward proxy), allows a remote, unauthenticated attacker to direct the appliance to issue requests on their behalf, reach internal functionality, and perform unauthorized operations. The vulnerability has been designated **CVE-2026-102255** and has been rated *critical* with a CVSS score of **10.0**.
- [CVE-2026-102256](https://www.cve.org/cverecord?id=CVE-2026-102256): An OS command injection vulnerability in the SMA1000 appliance allows a remote attacker authenticated as an administrator to execute arbitrary OS commands, resulting in remote code execution. The vulnerability has been designated **CVE-2026-102256** and has been rated *high* with a CVSS score of **7.8**.
- [CVE-2026-102257](https://www.cve.org/cverecord?id=CVE-2026-102257): A Zip Slip vulnerability in the SMA1000 Appliance Management Console (AMC) interface allows an authenticated, high-privilege attacker to extract files outside the intended destination directory using a specially crafted archive, resulting in remote code execution. The vulnerability has been designated **CVE-2026-102257** and has been rated *high* with a CVSS score of **7.2**.
- [CVE-2026-102258](https://www.cve.org/cverecord?id=CVE-2026-102258): A stored cross-site scripting (XSS) vulnerability in the SMA1000 Appliance Management Console (AMC) allows a remote attacker authenticated as an administrator to store and potentially execute arbitrary JavaScript code in the AMC. The vulnerability has been designated **CVE-2026-102258** and has been rated *medium* with a CVSS score of **6.1**.

**The following versions are affected**

- SonicWall SMA1000 (models 6210, 7210, and 8200v) 12.4: Versions 12.4.3-03526 (platform-hotfix) and older
- SonicWall SMA1000 (models 6210, 7210, and 8200v) 12.5: Versions 12.5.0-02952 (platform-hotfix) and older

Note: These vulnerabilities do not affect SSL-VPN running on SonicWall firewalls or the SMA 100 Series product line.

SonicWall [disclosed](https://psirt.global.sonicwall.com/vuln-detail/SNWLID-2024-0015) a vulnerability that affects SonicOS management access and SSLVPN software on SonicWall Gen 5, Gen 6, in addition to Gen 7 devices running SonicOS version 7.0.1-5035 or earlier.

[CVE-2024-40766](https://nvd.nist.gov/vuln/detail/CVE-2024-40766) is rated ***critical*** with CVSS score of **9.3**, and potentially allows for unauthorized resource access by an attacker.

### What is the impact? [#](https://www.runzero.com#what-is-the-impact)

Successful exploitation of this vulnerability potentially results in unauthorized resource access and in some cases could lead to a DoS after causing vulnerable devices to crash.

### Are updates or workarounds available? [#](https://www.runzero.com#are-updates-or-workarounds-available)

SonicWall recommends restricting management access to trusted sources or disabling WAN management from the public Internet. Additionally, SonicWall has released updated firmware which is available for download from [mysonicwall.com](https://mysonicwall.com).

### How to find potentially vulnerable systems with runZero [#](https://www.runzero.com#how-to-find-potentially-vulnerable-systems-with-runZero)

From the [Asset Inventory](https://console.runzero.com/inventory), use the following query to locate systems running potentially vulnerable software:

`hw:"SonicWall"`
