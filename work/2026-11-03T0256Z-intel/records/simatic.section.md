VulnCheck says one of its canaries caught an attacker exploiting CVE-2026-58115 on 2026-10-30 with a single POST to the Node-RED /flows endpoint that deployed a flow downloading and running a Linux bot, and that the same address produced 16 events against this CVE by 2026-11-01, some deploying persistent http-in web-shell flows; it did not retrieve the bot and reads the traffic as opportunistic mass scanning ([VulnCheck, 2026-11-01](https://www.vulncheck.com/blog/exploitation-targeting-node-red)). The target was a canary, not a Siemens device, and CISA's catalog did not list the flaw when VulnCheck wrote ([VulnCheck, 2026-11-01](https://www.vulncheck.com/blog/exploitation-targeting-node-red)); The Register relays the finding ([The Register, 2026-11-02](https://www.theregister.com/security/2026/11/02/max-severity-bug-in-widely-used-ot-programming-tool-exploited-to-deliver-linux-malware/5307086)). The earlier statement that no exploitation was reported no longer holds.
