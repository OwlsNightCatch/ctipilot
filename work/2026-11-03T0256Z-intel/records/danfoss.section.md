On 2026-11-02 refrigeration failures struck stores in Belgium, Luxembourg, the Netherlands, Sweden, Ireland and Estonia, and Finland according to The Guardian; BleepingComputer counts more than 115 affected stores from company statements, and several companies confirmed they had been hacked ([BleepingComputer, 2026-11-02](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/hackers-disrupt-refrigeration-systems-at-european-supermarkets/); [The Guardian, 2026-11-02](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/nov/02/supermarket-freezers-across-europe-hit-by-second-suspected-cyber-attack)). Sabcobel's chief executive says the units' evaporators were switched to defrost mode ([The Guardian, 2026-11-02](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/nov/02/supermarket-freezers-across-europe-hit-by-second-suspected-cyber-attack)) and, per BleepingComputer, that the attacker used the same vulnerability as in France ([BleepingComputer, 2026-11-02](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/hackers-disrupt-refrigeration-systems-at-european-supermarkets/)); Danfoss says customers who had not updated their software were affected ([Euronews, 2026-11-02](https://www.euronews.com/2026/11/02/cyberattack-hits-belgian-supermarket-fridges-and-freezers)), and in Estonia attackers reached the control computer of an older Danfoss system with outdated software (translated from German) ([Tagesschau, 2026-11-02](https://www.tagesschau.de/wirtschaft/technologie/cyberangriff-supermaerkte-100.html)). The manufacturer, controller models and initial access method of the new wave remain unknown ([CyberInsider, 2026-11-02](https://cyberinsider.com/supermarket-refrigeration-cyberattacks-belgium-sweden/)). Coop Switzerland told 20 Minuten that corresponding attempts were registered without success, and Migros reports no indication of an attack ([20 Minuten, 2026-11-02](https://www.20min.ch/story/seit-mitte-oktober-mysterioese-hacking-welle-trifft-europaeische-supermaerkte-103660687)). The head of Sweden's national cybersecurity centre says it is working on incidents around the country and that Sweden has quite a lot of exposed operational technology devices ([The Guardian, 2026-11-02](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/nov/02/supermarket-freezers-across-europe-hit-by-second-suspected-cyber-attack)).
