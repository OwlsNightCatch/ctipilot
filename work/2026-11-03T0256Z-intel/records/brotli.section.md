Patchstack reports that WordPress ships no Brotli of its own but links the operating system's libbrotli through libcurl, so one unauthenticated POST to xmlrpc.php can take down a PHP worker on a stock install: the pingback method makes WordPress fetch an attacker-named URL and decode the Brotli reply ([Patchstack, 2026-10-30](https://patchstack.com/articles/wordpress-doesnt-ship-brotli-its-still-exposed-to-cve-2026-111497/)). A laboratory demonstration killed a worker or pinned it at full CPU, its author says no code execution is included, and Patchstack says no public chain aims at PHP; the fix is a patched libbrotli package, and the official wordpress:latest image still carried a vulnerable one when Patchstack wrote ([Patchstack, 2026-10-30](https://patchstack.com/articles/wordpress-doesnt-ship-brotli-its-still-exposed-to-cve-2026-111497/)).
