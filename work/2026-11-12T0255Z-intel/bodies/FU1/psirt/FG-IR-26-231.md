# Improper privilege management

## Summary

An improper privilege management vulnerability \[CWE-269\] in FortiAnalyzer, FortiAnalyzer Cloud, FortiManager and FortiManager Cloud may allow an authenticated privileged attacker to elevate their privileges via crafted JSON-RPC request.
