# Incorrect authorization

## Summary

An Incorrect Authorization vulnerability \[CWE-863\] in FortiAuthenticator may allow an attacker to bypass authentication and gain unauthorized access. Full impersonation however requires the attacker to obtain pre-required id and data via other means.

| Version | Affected | Solution | 
|---|---|---|
| FortiAuthenticator 8.0 | 8.0.0 through 8.0.3 | Upgrade to 8.0.4 or above | 
| FortiAuthenticator 6.6 | 6.6.0 through 6.6.10 | Upgrade to 6.6.11 or above |
