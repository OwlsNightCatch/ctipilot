# Numeric truncation error

## Summary

A Numeric Truncation Error vulnerability \[cwe-197\] in FortiOS and FortiSASE bgp daemon may allow a configured ibgp neighbor to send malicious/malformed attributes to all connected routers via a specially crafted request

| Version | Affected | Solution |

| FortiOS 7.6 | 7.6.0 through 7.6.7 | Upgrade to 7.6.8 or above | 
| FortiOS 7.4 | 7.4.0 through 7.4.12 | Upgrade to 7.4.13 or above | 
| FortiOS 7.2 | 7.2.0 through 7.2.13 | Upgrade to 7.2.14 or above |
