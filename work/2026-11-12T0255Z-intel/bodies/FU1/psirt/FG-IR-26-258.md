# Allocation of resources without limits or throttling in fastapi

## Summary

An Allocation of Resources Without Limits or Throttling vulnerability \[CWE-770\] in FortiAuthenticator may allow an unauthenticated attacker to exhaust system resources, potentially resulting in a denial of service (DoS).

| Version | Affected | Solution | 
|---|---|---|
| FortiAuthenticator 8.0 | 8.0.0 through 8.0.3 | Upgrade to 8.0.4 or above | 
| FortiAuthenticator 6.6 | 6.6.0 through 6.6.10 | Upgrade to 6.6.11 or above |
