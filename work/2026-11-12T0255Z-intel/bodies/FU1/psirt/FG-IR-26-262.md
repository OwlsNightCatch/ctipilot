# Insufficiently protected credentials

## Summary

An insufficiently protected credentials vulnerability\[CWE-522\] in FortiAuthenticator may allow an unauthenticated attacker to obtain sensitive information via HTTPS requests.

| Version | Affected | Solution | 
|---|---|---|
| FortiAuthenticator 8.0 | 8.0.0 through 8.0.3 | Upgrade to 8.0.4 or above | 
| FortiAuthenticator 6.6 | 6.6.0 through 6.6.10 | Upgrade to 6.6.11 or above |
