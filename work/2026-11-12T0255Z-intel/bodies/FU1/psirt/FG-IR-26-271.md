# Authentication bypass

## Summary

A Use of Hard-coded Password \[CWE-259\] vulnerability in FortiAuthenticator may allow an unauthenticated attacker to bypass credential validation in certain circumstances outside of the attacker's control and gain unauthorized access.

| Version | Affected | Solution | 
|---|---|---|
| FortiAuthenticator 8.0 | 8.0.0 through 8.0.3 | Upgrade to 8.0.4 or above | 
| FortiAuthenticator 6.6 | 6.6.0 through 6.6.10 | Upgrade to 6.6.11 or above |
