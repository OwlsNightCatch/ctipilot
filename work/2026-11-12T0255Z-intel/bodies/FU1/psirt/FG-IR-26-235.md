# Authorization bypass through user-controlled key

## Summary

An Authorization Bypass Through User-Controlled Key vulnerability \[CWE-639\] in FortiManager may allow a remote authenticated attacker with admin permission to elevate their privileges to root via crafted HTTP requests.

| Version | Affected | Solution | 
|---|---|---|
| FortiManager 8.0 | 8.0.0 through 8.0.1 | Upgrade to 8.0.2 or above | 
| FortiManager 7.6 | 7.6.0 through 7.6.7 | Upgrade to 7.6.8 or above | 
| FortiManager 7.4 | 7.4.0 through 7.4.11 | Upgrade to 7.4.12 or above | 
| FortiManager 7.2 | Not affected | Not Applicable |
