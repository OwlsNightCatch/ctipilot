# Incorrect authorization

## Summary

An Incorrect Authorization vulnerability \[CWE-863\], an Improper Privilege Management vulnerability \[CWE-269\] and an Authentication Bypass by Spoofing vulnerability \[CWE-290\] in FortiManager, FortiManager Cloud, FortiAnalyzer, FortiAnalyzer Cloud, and FortiAnalyzer-BigData may allow an authenticated administrator to escalate their privileges, bypass authorization controls or impersonate internal services via specially crafted requests to the GUI.
