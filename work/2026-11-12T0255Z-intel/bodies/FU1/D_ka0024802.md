TrendAI Protection and Detection Against Exploitation

First and foremost, it is always recommended that users apply vendor-specific patches when they are available. In this case, customers are recommended to update to the latest version of PHP as recommended by the source maintainers.

Summary

On October 21, 2026, the PHP development team announced two critical (CVSSv4 9.2) and interrelated vulnerabilities in PHP (CVE-2026-109618/CVE-2026-109619).

This vulnerability allows unauthorized attackers to access the network via HTTP, enabling remote code execution without authentication, potentially leading to issues that significantly impact confidentiality, integrity, and availability.

This article explains the impact of the vulnerabilities on TrendAI products and services and will be detailed with further mitigation and remediation information as it becomes available.
