## 
[Chrome for Android Update](https://chromereleases.googleblog.com/2026/11/chrome-for-android-update_01533302390.html)

Monday, November 9, 2026

Hi, everyone! We've just released **Chrome 157 (157.0.8103.50)** for Android. It'll become [available on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.android.chrome) over the next few days.

This release includes stability and performance improvements. You can see a full list of the changes in the [Git log](https://chromium.googlesource.com/chromium/src/+log/157.0.8103.46..157.0.8103.50?pretty=fuller&n=10000). If you find a new issue, please let us know by [filing a bug](https://code.google.com/p/chromium/issues/entry?template=Android%20Issue).

Android releases contain the same security fixes as their corresponding [Desktop releases](https://chromereleases.googleblog.com/2026/11/stable-channel-update-for-desktop_018223192.html) (Windows & Mac: 157.0.8103.50/.51 Linux: 157.0.8103.50) unless otherwise noted.
