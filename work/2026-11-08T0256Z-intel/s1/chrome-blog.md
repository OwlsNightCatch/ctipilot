## 
[Stable Channel Update for ChromeOS / ChromeOS Flex](https://chromereleases.googleblog.com/2026/11/stable-channel-update-for-chromeos.html)

M-156, ChromeOS version 16836.32.0 (Browser version 156.0.8078.93）has rolled out to ChromeOS devices on the Stable channel.

If you find new issues, please let us know one of the following ways:

1. [File a bug](https://bugs.chromium.org/p/chromium/issues/list)
2. Visit our ChromeOS communities
3. General: [Chromebook Help Community](https://support.google.com/chromebook/community/?hl=en&gpf=%23!forum%2Fchromebook-central)
4. Beta Specific: [ChromeOS Beta Help Community](https://support.google.com/chromeos-beta/community)
5. [Report an issue or send feedback on Chrome](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/95315?hl=en&co=GENIE.Platform%3DDesktop)
6. Interested in switching channels? [Find out how.](https://support.google.com/chromebook/answer/1086915)
