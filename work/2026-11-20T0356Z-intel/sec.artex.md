The European Commission confirmed on 2026-11-19 that an AI-generated cyber campaign hit its internal systems: its spokesperson said the incident was detected on 2026-10-22 and confirmed on 2026-10-26, that several applications were taken offline as a protective measure and most are restored, that there is no evidence of a data breach affecting Commission staff as a whole and that it is not aware of a ransom request, and the Commission names no tool or attacker and declines to attribute or size the campaign ([European Commission, 2026-11-19](https://audiovisual.ec.europa.eu/en/media/video/I-298222)). POLITICO, citing an anonymous EU official, reports that CERT-EU detected earlier this month that international hackers used ARTEX, that the staff HR platform Sysper and the medical-reimbursement system MyPMO were offline for more than 10 days, and that investigators have not yet established whether staff data was compromised ([POLITICO, 2026-11-19](https://www.politico.eu/newsletter/brussels-playbook/cyberattack-hits-the-commission/)). The Commission has not confirmed the ARTEX link, and the reporting does not establish whether ARTEX provided entry or was only used to find weaknesses ([Cyber Security News, 2026-11-19](https://cybersecuritynews.com/european-commission-cyberattack/)).
