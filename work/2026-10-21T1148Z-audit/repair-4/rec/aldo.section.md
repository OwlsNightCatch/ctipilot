In practice the flaw is not a no-credentials, no-foothold remote bug: Microsoft's realistic scenario is a malicious or compromised insider with existing access and identity material, and an external attacker would first need access to the internal network and valid identity context ([MSRC, 2026-05-18](https://msrc.microsoft.com/update-guide/en-US/vulnerability/CVE-2026-42822)). The advisory does not say that cloud-managed Azure was already protected while manual stacks were not: the affected product is ALDO, and its fix is version 2604 or later, applied as a full system update through the Azure portal. No source says ALDO is common in German or Swiss federal administrations; Microsoft describes it as a restricted offering for approved customers ([MSRC, 2026-05-18](https://msrc.microsoft.com/update-guide/en-US/vulnerability/CVE-2026-42822)).
