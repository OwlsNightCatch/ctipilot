The report covers more than 31,000 incidents and 22,000 confirmed breaches in 145 countries ([Verizon 2026 DBIR](https://www.verizon.com/business/resources/T1f0/reports/2026-dbir-data-breach-investigations-report.pdf)) that date from November 2024 to October 2025 ([Help Net Security, 2026-05-20](https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/05/20/verizon-2026-dbir-findings/)), so its third-party growth reflects 2025 data and says nothing about campaigns active in May 2026 such as TeamPCP or Mini Shai-Hulud. The 26% is the share of KEV-listed vulnerabilities fully remediated by the organizations polled, not a share of organizations bound by BOD 22-01 missing deadlines, and 23% is the share of third-party organizations that fully remediated their MFA issues ([Help Net Security, 2026-05-20](https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/05/20/verizon-2026-dbir-findings/)). Exploitation rose from 20% to 31% in one year rather than over three cycles, and credential abuse fell from 22% to 13%, a drop Verizon partly ascribes to tracking pretexting separately ([Verizon 2026 DBIR](https://www.verizon.com/business/resources/T1f0/reports/2026-dbir-data-breach-investigations-report.pdf)).
