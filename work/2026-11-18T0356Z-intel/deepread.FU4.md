### Contradictions and corrections (against the existing entry and the first pass)

1. **Start date for the Optimizely files (existing entry vs the sources).** The entry (via Ohey) says the Optimizely customer project files gained the loader from 2026-11-13. Report URI puts the first Optimizely injection at 2026-11-16 04:21 UTC (last clean BBC capture 02:21 UTC) and the 13-to-16 November wave in the Kissmetrics, StorifyMe and Minute Media scripts only; Hudson Rock's own capture timeline agrees (its 13 November captures are Kissmetrics and StorifyMe, its first Optimizely capture is the BBC at 2026-11-16 04:31 UTC). Ohey's sentence (quoted above) lists Optimizely among the scripts from 13 November, which the capture timelines do not support. Per Report URI the first Optimizely injection (BBC, 04:21 UTC) loaded the earlier wave's router-loader host, and the clipper script was first seen on bbc.com at 08:28 UTC (card skimmer 13:43 UTC). The action item's "since 2026-11-13" is right for Kissmetrics, StorifyMe and minute-ly (router loader only, per the entry's own Hudson Rock statement) and too early for card and cookie exposure through Optimizely, where Report URI dates the card skimmer to 13:43 UTC on 2026-11-16 and says "treat cards entered on 16 November as potentially exposed".
2. **First pass: "The Hudson Rock 21-property count is a subset of Report URI's 178" is not supported.** Report URI says the earlier wave's sites "isn't included above". The Optimizely-served properties Hudson Rock names (BBC, Zoom, GitLab, HSBC UAE, Hootsuite, Virgin Media O2, Stamps.com, Tricentis; CNN, Salesforce, Mutual of Omaha, Fox News from Ohey's table) do appear in Report URI's list; the Kissmetrics/StorifyMe-served ones (securitybank.com, faz.net, smh.com.au, vidaxl, and the University of Basel) are outside it: Report URI names the first four only as examples in its timeline and never names the University of Basel. ### Contradictions and corrections (against the existing entry and the first pass)

1. **Does any source tie Fireworks' second incident (2026-10-23 to 2026-10-30) to the Cursor marketplace compromise? No.** Cursor's report says the chain began with "an access key we had issued to Fireworks" that "was exposed by Fireworks", without a date or an incident number; it links its words "exposed by Fireworks" to the Fireworks bulletins listing. No Fireworks section (10-10, 11-09, 11-17) mentions Cursor, Uber or Notion (searched in the saved body), and Uber's letter does not name Fireworks. Dates only: the marketplace placement on 2026-10-29 falls inside the second incident's window, and the 11-17 text says both incidents exposed secrets for a small number of customers, so the page does not discriminate; any mapping of the Cursor key to an incident is inference. The entry's existing statement that the Fireworks bulletin "does not mention Cursor" remains true of the 11-17 section.
2. **Uber vs the two incidents (inference from dates).** Uber's cover letter dates the credential use to 2026-10-07 and the provider's notice to 2026-10-09, both before the second incident began on 2026-10-23, so if the provider is Fireworks (Uber's spokesperson says so to TechCrunch) Uber's case lies in the first incident's window (ends 2026-10-07).
