## Summary

BRONZE SANDCASTLE is a threat group likely working under the direction of the leadership of the People's Republic of China (PRC). The group has been observed since mid-2025, and is associated with DLL side-loading attacks that execute a modified meterpreter payload. The group has been observed targeting organizations consistent with PRC intelligence gathering requirements in Asia and Africa.
