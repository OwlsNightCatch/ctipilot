**Mail Assure and SpamExperts Hosted Cloud Security Incident**

On November 16, we informed customers about a service disruption affecting Mail Assure and SpamExperts Hosted Cloud. Our earlier update said the disruption came from platform configuration and service dependencies and we believed we had remediated the issue. As we continued our investigation, we determined that the disruption was caused by a security incident affecting the Mail Assure and SpamExperts Hosted Cloud environment.

**Incident Overview**: We are aware of unauthorized access to one of the servers in the Mail Assure and SpamExperts Hosted Cloud. **We have taken steps to mitigate the threat and are continuing to harden our services to reduce the risk of recurrence.**The investigation is ongoing, but at this time we do not have evidence that customer data was compromised, including access to tenants’ mailboxes.

**This is unrelated to the N-central incident.** We have proactively disabled UI and API access while our investigation continues and remediation work is underway. Email flow and spam filtering in both Mail Assure and SpamExperts Hosted Cloud continue to operate unaffected.

We will continue to provide updates through the [N-able Uptime page](https://uptime.n-able.com/) as additional information becomes available.

If you need additional support, please contact N-able Technical Support. Thank you for your continued patience, trust, and partnership.

*   ###### Identified

Nov 17, 2026 13:33:42 UTC
N-able® has identified the root cause of the issue, and a fix is still being developed.

The issue was traced to a combination of platform configuration and service dependencies within the Mail Assure & Spam Experts Hosted Cloud environment. Remediation required rebuilding affected infrastructure and updating several platform components.

Core mail flow has remained available. Supporting services, including the management portal, quarantine, logging, and mailbox synchronization, have been undergoing restoration and validation.

**N-central Critical Security Update – 2026.4 HF3 Available**

Valued Customer, As part of our continued response to the non-responsible communication of critical vulnerabilities affecting N-central, **N-able has released N-central 2026.4 HF3**, which provides additional security updates and hardening measures.

HF3 builds on the protections delivered in HF2 and includes further improvements across authentication and access controls, Agent and Probe communications, file handling, server components, legacy APIs, and user management.

**The vulnerabilities announced on November 9 and November 12 are specific to N-central. No other N-able products are at risk from these vulnerabilities.**

**N-central On-Premises Environments:**

*   **Action Required:** Please upgrade your N-central server to 2026.4 HF3 as soon as possible.
*   **Download information:**Software Downloads: [SD - 000145](https://me.n-able.com/s/softwaredownloads/a9jVy0000000LXVIA2/sd-000145)
*   **N-central 2026.4 HF3 Release Notes:**[LINK](https://status.n-able.com/2026/11/16/n-central-2026-4-hf3-release-notes/)
*   **N-central Upgrade User Guide:**[https://documentation.n-able.com/N-central/userguide/Content/ReleaseDocs/Install_Config/InstallConfig_Upgrade_Step_2.htm](https://documentation.n-able.com/N-central/userguide/Content/ReleaseDocs/Install_Config/InstallConfig_Upgrade_Step_2.htm)
*   **N-central Legacy Version User Guide:**[https://documentation.n-able.com/N-central/userguide/Content/ReleaseDocs/Release_Notes/upgrade_path.htm](https://documentation.n-able.com/N-central/userguide/Content/ReleaseDocs/Release_Notes/upgrade_path.htm)
*   **Updates to Agents and Probes are also part of this security update.** See [How to upgrade Agents and Probes on N-able N-central version 2026.4 HF1 and above](https://me.n-able.com/s/article/How-to-upgrade-Agents-and-Probes-on-N-able-N-central-version-2026-4-HF1-and-above) for additional guidance.

**N-central Hosted Environments:**

*   **No server-side action is required.** N-able is applying HF3 to N-central Hosted environments.
*   HF3 includes additional security updates and hardening measures.
*   **Updates to Agents and Probes are also part of this security update.** See [How to upgrade Agents and Probes on N-able N-central version 2026.4 HF1 and above](https://me.n-able.com/s/article/How-to-upgrade-Agents-and-Probes-on-N-able-N-central-version-2026-4-HF1-and-above) for additional guidance.

**Security Improvements in HF3:**

HF3 includes additional security improvements across the following areas:

*   **Authentication and access control:**Added authentication and permission checks to additional server operations.
*   **Agent, Probe, and appliance security:**Strengthened appliance authentication and validation of requests between Agents, Probes, and appliances, including appliance session handling.
*   **File and import handling:** Added additional validation for file publishing, file paths, and XML imports.
*   **Server hardening:**Further hardened server components, including how the server handles incoming requests and makes outbound connections.
*   **Legacy APIs:** Removed the configuration option for legacy APIs. Legacy APIs are now permanently blocked.
*   **User management:** Improved how user accounts are managed and secured.

**Open Issues:**

*   **Remote control is not available.** Remote control functionality other than Take Control is disabled. Take Control continues to work as expected.
*   **Intune integration is blocked.** The Intune integration is currently blocked.

We appreciate your continued partnership and patience as we have worked through these updates. N-able remains committed to taking the actions necessary to help protect N-central environments.

If you need assistance applying HF3 or have questions regarding this update, please contact Support through N-able Me.

**November 12 Update: Critical N-central Security Update**

**Update: Following additional security disclosures, N-able is taking further precautionary action to protect N-central customers.** As the situation continues to evolve, we will share any further updates and guidance here.

**We are restricting certain N-central features and integrations.**These measures may affect some functionality, but we believe taking additional precautions while remediation is completed is the right action to protect our partners and their customers.

Importantly, **we have seen no signs of compromise. We are continuing to monitor closely and will provide updates as needed.**

**N-central HOSTED Customers**

**N-able has applied these temporary protections directly to Hosted environments. No server-side action is required from Hosted customers.**

*   Legacy API is turned off by default in the product.
*   Intune integration has been turned off while additional protections are implemented and validated. This is a precautionary step.
*   Hosted customers should continue to follow our previous guidance to upgrade Agents and Probes.

**N-central ON-PREMISES Customers**

**Ensure Legacy API access is disabled in your environment.**

*   Legacy API access is disabled by default. If you enabled it, follow these instructions: **[How to Disable Legacy API settings on On-Premises N-central servers](https://me.n-able.com/s/article/How-to-Disable-Legacy-API-settings-on-On-premise-N-central-servers)**
*   As a precaution, if you are running the Intune integration, we have temporarily deactivated it for you. If we were unable to execute this, our support team will make contact with you. This is a precautionary step.
*   On-Premises customers should also continue to follow the previously communicated HF2 guidance, including upgrading their Agents and Probes

**What Happens Next** Our teams are actively assessing the new disclosures, developing additional remediation, and working to safely restore affected functionality. Protecting our partners and their customers remains our highest priority, and we will continue to take precautionary action where necessary.

**This remains a fast-moving situation.** Please continue to monitor this uptime dashboard.
