CISA added both CVE-2026-116606 and CVE-2026-111560 to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog on 2026-11-10 "based on evidence of active exploitation" ([CISA, 2026-11-10](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/alerts/2026/11/10/cisa-adds-six-known-exploited-vulnerabilities-catalog)). The catalog describes the two as chainable, the first letting an unauthenticated remote attacker restore a crafted backup archive and the second letting an authenticated administrator run commands as root through a crafted timezone value, and marks both for forensic triage ([CISA, 2026-11-10](https://www.cisa.gov/sites/default/files/feeds/known_exploited_vulnerabilities.json)). The listing makes CVE-2026-111560 exploited as well; Barracuda's own statement of 2026-11-09 still names no flaw, and no source says how many organizations were hit or what the attacker did afterwards.
