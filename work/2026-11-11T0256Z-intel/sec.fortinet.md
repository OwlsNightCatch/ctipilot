Fortinet published the advisories it had withheld from the October hardening release on 2026-11-10. They name unauthenticated FortiMail flaws that may allow code or command execution through crafted requests (CVE-2026-126147, CVSS 9.8) ([Fortinet PSIRT, 2026-11-10](https://fortiguard.fortinet.com/psirt/FG-IR-26-199)) and through HA join-request packets (CVE-2026-126148, 9.8) ([Fortinet PSIRT, 2026-11-10](https://fortiguard.fortinet.com/psirt/FG-IR-26-200)), an unauthenticated SQL injection (CVE-2026-126164, 9.4) that Fortinet remediated in FortiMail Cloud on 2026-10-05 ([Fortinet PSIRT, 2026-11-10](https://fortiguard.fortinet.com/psirt/FG-IR-26-216)), and two IBE webmail flaws that let an attacker take over an IBE recipient account (CVE-2026-126156, 9.1) ([Fortinet PSIRT, 2026-11-10](https://fortiguard.fortinet.com/psirt/FG-IR-26-208)) or obtain an authenticated session as one (CVE-2026-126155, 9.1) ([Fortinet PSIRT, 2026-11-10](https://fortiguard.fortinet.com/psirt/FG-IR-26-207)). A FortiAuthenticator hard-coded-password bypass (CVE-2026-126257, 9.0) ([Fortinet PSIRT, 2026-11-10](https://fortiguard.fortinet.com/psirt/FG-IR-26-271)), a FortiOS and FortiSASE OSPF flaw (CVE-2026-126253, 9.3) ([Fortinet PSIRT, 2026-11-10](https://fortiguard.fortinet.com/psirt/FG-IR-26-267)) and unauthenticated code execution through the FortiManager FGFM port (CVE-2026-126261, 9.0) ([Fortinet PSIRT, 2026-11-10](https://fortiguard.fortinet.com/psirt/FG-IR-26-275)) complete the set. The fixed versions are the October builds and no Fortinet page states exploitation.
