PK Softech said on its homepage on 2026-11-10, "because of media speculation", that no concrete ransom demand has reached it and that it has at no time contacted the attackers; its investigation continues ([PK Softech, 2026-11-10](https://pksoftech.ch/de/newsreader/Information-zum-Cyberangriff-auf-die-PKSoftechAG)). A spokeswoman of the State Secretariat for Security Policy told Keystone-SDA the same day that the federal bodies handling the incident know of no concrete ransom demand addressed to them and that the Confederation paid no ransom, that the supplier did receive a demand but one "not made concrete with an amount or deadline" (translated from German), and that the Secretariat still has no indication that data from the attack was published on the darkweb; Publica said it also received no demand, is not in contact with the hackers and does not know whether other organisations or individuals are being extorted, which the Federal Prosecutor's Office is investigating ([watson.ch, 2026-11-10](https://www.watson.ch/schweiz/datenschutz/664513749-nach-daten-klau-keine-loesegeldforderung-von-hackern-an-publica-softwarelieferan)). This qualifies the press report that Akira demands ransom: neither the supplier nor the federal authorities confirm a concrete demand, and the Akira attribution and the data volume remain press-sourced.
