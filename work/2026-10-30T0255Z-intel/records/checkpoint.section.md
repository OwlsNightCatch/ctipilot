Check Point's sk1000302 carries a new note of 2026-10-29 at 11:30 GMT, "Released Hotfix for R81.10" ([Check Point, 2026-10-29](https://support.checkpoint.com/results/sk/sk1000302)). R81.10 is an end-of-support gateway release that the sk still lists as affected, so a gateway on it now has a hotfix to install, with the same portal and VPN scope and the same cp-httpd.conf mitigation until it is in. The other hotfixes and the exploitation statement are unchanged.
