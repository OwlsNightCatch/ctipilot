HPE's revision 3 of bulletin HPESBNW05171, dated 2026-10-29, updates its affected and resolution sections to exceptionally include ClearPass 6.12.8 ([HPE Networking, 2026-10-29](https://csaf.arubanetworking.hpe.com/2026/hpe_networking_-_hpesbnw05171.json)). ClearPass 6.12.8 and below is now listed as affected, and a ClearPass 6.12.8 hotfix patch for CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619 is named as the fix, downloadable from the HPE Networking Support Portal ([HPE Networking, 2026-10-29](https://csaf.arubanetworking.hpe.com/2026/hpe_networking_-_hpesbnw05171.json)). The 6.14.1 and 6.11.16 entries are unchanged, and the bulletin still says nothing about exploitation of ClearPass.
