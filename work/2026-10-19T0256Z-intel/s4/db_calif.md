# Hack exposes data on millions of California students

Posted on [October 17, 2026 October 17, 2026](https://databreaches.net/2026/10/17/hack-exposes-data-on-millions-of-california-students/) by [Dissent](https://databreaches.net/author/dissent/)

Eric He and Tyler Katzenberger report:

> Personal information about millions of California students was stolen in a hack of a non-profit organization that contracts with the state’s Department of Education, officials announced Friday.
> 
> 
> The names, addresses, demographic information and disability status of all 7 million students enrolled in California public schools over the past three school years were exposed in the breach, the CDE said. The hack targeted the **Education Testing Service**, which administers standardized tests for across the U.S and globally. In California, it manages statewide assessments such as [the Smarter Balanced exam](https://subscriber.politicopro.com/article/2026/10/california-test-scores-inch-up-but-pre-pandemic-levels-remain-elusive-01119685) and English proficiency tests.
> 
> 
> An “unauthorized party used a compromised login credential” to copy files containing the student data between May 22 and Sept. 18, according to CDE officials.

Read more at [Politico](https://www.politico.com/news/2026/10/16/hack-exposes-data-on-millions-of-california-students-01124018).
