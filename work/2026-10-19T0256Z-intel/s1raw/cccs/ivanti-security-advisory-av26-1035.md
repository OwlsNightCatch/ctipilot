**Serial number:** AV26-1035

**Date:** October 13, 2026

**Updated:** October 16, 2026

As of October 13, 2026, Ivanti is affected by vulnerabilities in the following products:

- Ivanti Connect Secure
	
  - Version 25.1.1.1 and prior
  - Version 25.2.2 and prior
  - Version 22.8R2.4 and prior
- Ivanti Neurons Patch for Intune
	
  - Versions prior to September 16, 2026
- Ivanti Xtraction
	
  - Version 2026.2.1 and prior

On October 13, 2026, Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency (CISA) added CVE-2026-104935 to their Known Exploited Vulnerabilities (KEV) Database.

**Update 1**

Open-source reporting indicates that CVE-2026-111357 is being exploited in the wild.

The Cyber Centre encourages users and administrators to review the provided web links and apply any necessary updates as they become available.
