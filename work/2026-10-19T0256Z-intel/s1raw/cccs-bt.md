**Serial number:** AV26-1054

**Date:** October 16, 2026

**Updated:** October 16, 2026

As of October 15, 2026, BeyondTrust is affected by a vulnerability in the following product:

- Remote Support
	
  - Prior to or equal to 26.2.3

Open-source reporting indicates that CVE-2026-102764 is being exploited in the wild.

## Update 1

On October 16, 2026, Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency (CISA) added CVE-2026-102764 to their Known Exploited Vulnerabilities (KEV) Database.

The Cyber Centre encourages users and administrators to review the provided web links and apply any necessary updates as they become available.
