The one-time re-authentication is a consequence of the upgrade, not a step to force: with the fix, a firewall that uses authentication override cookies regenerates the cookie with a more secure method, so GlobalProtect users re-authenticate once after the upgrade even when they hold a valid cookie ([Palo Alto Networks PSIRT, 2026-05-13](https://security.paloaltonetworks.com/CVE-2026-0257)). Cookie logins are routine where Authentication Override is enabled; in some intrusions Arctic Wolf saw a cookie-based authentication failure with the error Cannot decrypt cookie, followed quickly by a successful cookie-based authentication from the same source ([Arctic Wolf, 2026-06-11](https://arcticwolf.com/resources/blog/arctic-wolf-observes-increase-in-palo-alto-networks-globalprotect-authentication-bypass-exploitation-via-cve-2026-0257/)).
