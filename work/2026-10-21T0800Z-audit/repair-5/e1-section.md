Red Hat's advisories for the three flaws predate the catalog listing: Red Hat made CVE-2025-39682 public on 2025-09-05 ([Red Hat Product Security, 2025-09-05](https://access.redhat.com/security/cve/cve-2025-39682)), CVE-2025-39964 on 2025-10-13 ([Red Hat Product Security, 2025-10-13](https://access.redhat.com/security/cve/cve-2025-39964)) and CVE-2026-53266 on 2026-06-01 ([Red Hat Product Security, 2026-06-01](https://access.redhat.com/security/cve/cve-2026-53266)), and updated all three on 2026-09-19 with the statement that public exploits exist ([The Hacker News, 2026-09-19](https://thehackernews.com/2026/09/cisa-flags-three-linux-kernel.html)). The kernel.org ChangeLogs of the fixed releases list the fix commits that CISA's catalog record names for each CVE; they do not name the CVE ids ([CISA KEV catalog, 2026-09-18](https://www.cisa.gov/sites/default/files/feeds/known_exploited_vulnerabilities.json)).
