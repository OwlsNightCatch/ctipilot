Google's bulletin itself carries the exploitation note: "There are indications that CVE-2026-58704 may be under limited, targeted exploitation" ([Google, 2026-09-15](https://source.android.com/docs/security/bulletin/pixel/2026/2026-09-01)). TechCrunch's opening sentence, that the bug "was exploited in limited and targeted cyberattacks", is stronger than Google's wording ([TechCrunch, 2026-09-16](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/16/google-says-some-pixel-phone-owners-were-hacked-in-zero-day-attacks/)). ENISA's EU Vulnerability Database record describes a remote (proximal/adjacent) escalation of privilege that needs no user interaction and rates it CVSS 3.1 8.8 with an adjacent-network vector ([ENISA EU Vulnerability Database, 2026-09-15](https://euvd.enisa.europa.eu/vulnerability/EUVD-2026-79004)).
