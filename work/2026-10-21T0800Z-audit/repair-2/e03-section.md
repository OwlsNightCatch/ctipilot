Check Point's hunt for CVE-2026-50751 is a search of gateway logs for completed IKEv1 Quick Mode exchanges ("Key Install" events marked Quick Mode completion) over at least 7 May to 5 June 2026. Any such event recorded before the hotfix or mitigation is to be treated as suspicious and inspected for unrecognised users or source IP addresses, and Check Point offers an IPS protection for the public proof-of-concept that adds visibility into attempts and scanning without remediating the flaw ([Check Point, sk185033](https://support.checkpoint.com/results/sk/sk185033)). The compromise lookback from 7 May follows that criterion.
