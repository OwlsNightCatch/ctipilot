The CVSS 9.8 for CVE-2026-48611 and 8.0 for CVE-2026-48612 that heise reports are the CVE records' own CVSS 3.0 scores, assigned by HackerOne as numbering authority ([ENISA EUVD, 2026-06-12](https://euvd.enisa.europa.eu/vulnerability/EUVD-2026-36375); [ENISA EUVD, 2026-06-12](https://euvd.enisa.europa.eu/vulnerability/EUVD-2026-36380)); Pentest-Tools.com, which found both flaws, scores them 9.4 and 8.3 ([Pentest-Tools.com, 2026-06-08](https://pentest-tools.com/research/phpbb-authentication-bypass)). The CVE record for CVE-2026-48611 describes improper authentication checks in phpBB's OAuth implementation that allow account hijacking even when OAuth is not configured or enabled ([ENISA EUVD, 2026-06-12](https://euvd.enisa.europa.eu/vulnerability/EUVD-2026-36375)). That wording stands; what does not hold is disabling OAuth as a stop-gap, which leaves this flaw open.
