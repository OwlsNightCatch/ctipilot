Downloading a repository and running its commands is common in IT work, which is why the interview task raised no suspicion; once run, the infostealer secretly reads crypto wallets, stored login credentials and browser cookies, and NCSC notes the incident becomes particularly serious where sensitive documents are stored on the device ([NCSC-CH, 2026-06-09](https://www.bacs.admin.ch/en/26w23-en)). A device where the task ran has therefore lost its stored credentials and live sessions, and the sessions need revoking along with the passwords.
