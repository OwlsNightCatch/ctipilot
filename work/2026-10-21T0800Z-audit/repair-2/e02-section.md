NCSC's advice gives the discriminator and the lever. Correct booking details and a seemingly authentic sender do not make a message genuine: the tell is an unsolicited refund or urgent card verification to be completed through a link, and a refund never asks for login details or a QR-code scan. Verification belongs out of band, through the official app or website or a call to the hotel on an independently looked-up number, and a card whose details were entered should be blocked at once ([NCSC-CH, 2026-06-02](https://www.bacs.admin.ch/en/26w22-en)).
