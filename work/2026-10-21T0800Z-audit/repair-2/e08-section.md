The first FortiBleed figures come from three researchers. Bob Diachenko found a server holding what appeared to be valid Fortinet VPN credentials, Hudson Rock counted 73,932 unique firewall URLs across 194 countries affecting 21,632 unique domains, and Kevin Beaumont, who confirmed the authenticity of some of the admin logins and passwords, put the dataset at roughly 75,000 Fortinet devices ([BleepingComputer, 2026-06-18](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/fortibleed-leak-exposes-fortinet-vpn-credentials-for-73-000-devices/)). SOCRadar's attribution of the campaign to the Lynx / INC ransomware group dates from a 29 June update to its report ([SOCRadar, 2026-06-16](https://socradar.io/blog/fortibleed-fortinet-firewalls-compromised/)), not from July. The FBI and Secret Service advise comparing firewall and VPN users and configuration against a known-good configuration, paying particular attention to accounts that were not on the device before ([FBI and U.S. Secret Service, 2026-10-06](https://www.ic3.gov/CSA/2026/261006.pdf)).
