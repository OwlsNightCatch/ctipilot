Palo Alto Networks revised its advisory on 2026-10-27 (16:40 UTC) to record that active exploitation has been observed targeting the Palo Alto Networks management interface exposed to the internet, with the severity updated to Critical and the urgency to Highest ([Palo Alto Networks, 2026-10-27](https://security.paloaltonetworks.com/CVE-2026-109618)). The vendor score is now CVSS-B 9.2 with high impact on confidentiality, integrity and availability when management is reachable from the internet, and 7.7 when it is reachable only from an adjacent network; the earlier text scored availability impact only (8.7) and knew of no exploitation. The advisory names no actor, victim or post-exploitation behaviour. The hotfix table is unchanged from the previous record: eleven builds released, nine due by 2026-10-30.
