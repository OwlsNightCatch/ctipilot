IDC Frontier's seventh report of 2026-10-27 says its investigation with an external security firm is complete and names the cause: a third party exploited a vulnerability in open-source software used in the authentication system of the IDCF Cloud Portal, the portal customers use to start virtual servers and check billing, and so entered that system (translated from Japanese; [IDC Frontier, 2026-10-27](https://www.idcf.jp/news/topics/20261027001)). The attacker then obtained administrator rights on the virtualisation platform through the management console, stopped customers' virtual servers and encrypted them; the software is not named. The provider finds no trace of customer data leaving the virtual servers, says the improper queries of portal registration data reached up to 71 customers, up from 48, and reports that backups are retrievable for 206 customers. It is rebuilding the portal in a new environment, replacing the affected authentication system with an external identity provider and limiting portal access to customer IP addresses with mandatory multi-factor authentication.
