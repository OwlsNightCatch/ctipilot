Inside IT reported on 2026-10-27 that SafePay has put the data stolen from Manno into the darknet, about 30 GB, and that the security firm Risikomonitor analysed it ([Inside IT, 2026-10-27](https://www.inside-it.ch/kritische-schweizer-infrastrukturen-attackiert-20261027)). Risikomonitor's analysis, relayed by Inside IT and not verified independently, finds files with about 5,300 e-mail addresses and more than 2,000 identifiable persons, social-assistance decisions with name, address, monthly amount, AHV numbers and health insurer from 2014 to December 2025, debt-enforcement extracts, naturalisation dossiers from 2018 and payment orders against tax debtors, and information on the whole population of 1,381 inhabitants ([Inside IT, 2026-10-27](https://www.inside-it.ch/kritische-schweizer-infrastrukturen-attackiert-20261027)). The commune told Inside IT it will make no statement. The leak-site listing of 2026-09-28 had described no dataset.
