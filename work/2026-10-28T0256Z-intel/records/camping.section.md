Netzwoche reported on 2026-10-27 that a second Swiss Camping.Care customer, Campingplatz Hüttenberg in canton Thurgau, has warned its guests of WhatsApp messages about existing bookings that contain real booking information and a link; the campsite's notice carries the page date 2026-10-23 ([Netzwoche, 2026-10-27](https://www.netzwoche.ch/news/2026-10-27/nach-angriff-ueber-php-sicherheitsluecke-nehmen-betrueger-camper-ins-visier); [Campingplatz Hüttenberg, 2026-10-23](https://www.camping-huettenberg.ch/erreichbarkeit-ueber-die-herbst-und-winterzeit-2/)). The notice says Camping.care informed the campsite of the messages, that Camping.care is investigating the cause and that booking data must be assumed to have been used without authorisation; it does not mention PHP, and Netzwoche points out that it does not say whether the attackers used one of the PHP flaws. The link to the PHP flaws therefore rests on SRF, Blick and the TCS statements, not on the supplier.
