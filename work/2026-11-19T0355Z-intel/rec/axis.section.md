CISA added CVE-2026-90436 to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog on 2026-11-18 on evidence of active exploitation, naming no product and no actor; the exploitation reported so far is against N-able N-central, not Confluence ([CISA, 2026-11-18](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/alerts/2026/11/18/cisa-adds-four-known-exploited-vulnerabilities-catalog)). Apache's announcement adds that the fault is parsed while the engine resolves the target service, before any per-service handler chain runs, so authentication handlers configured on a service do not protect against it, and recommends migrating from the end-of-life Axis 1 to a different SOAP engine such as Apache Axis 2/Java ([Apache Axis project, 2026-11-06](https://lists.apache.org/thread/8slf0j9gz35z56t0glt5h0ncwlbn4bdz)). Atlassian's advisory is unchanged and still says it cannot confirm whether instances were affected ([Atlassian, 2026-11-09](https://confluence.atlassian.com/security/cve-2026-90436-remote-code-execution-in-confluence-data-center-and-server-1874560038.html)).
