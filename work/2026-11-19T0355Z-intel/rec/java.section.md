Cisco published its product impact on 2026-11-18: Catalyst Center, Secure Network Analytics, Secure Workload and ten more products are affected, each with the fixed release still to be determined, Cisco offers no workaround, IOS XE and UCS Central are under investigation, and its PSIRT is aware of proof-of-concept code but not of malicious use ([Cisco, 2026-11-18](https://sec.cloudapps.cisco.com/security/center/content/CiscoSecurityAdvisory/cisco-sa-nov2026-java-rce-eCa6ss9z)). Fortinet says it is investigating the impact on its products and lists none as affected ([Fortinet, 2026-11-18](https://fortiguard.fortinet.com/psirt/FG-IR-26-278)). BACS's warning of 2026-11-18 asks operators to prioritise internet-reachable applications and central sign-in services, to include internal systems, to clarify other Java software such as banking software with their suppliers and to have externally operated services confirm the fix, and says an update removes no compromise that has already happened (translated from German) ([BACS, 2026-11-18](https://www.bacs.admin.ch/de/warnung-java-2-de)).
