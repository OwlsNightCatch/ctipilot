CISA added CVE-2024-10441, the remote code execution flaw of Synology-SA-24:20 to which Synology's ransomware notice points, to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog on 2026-11-18, on evidence of active exploitation; the alert names no actor or victim and does not mention C3RB3R, so the tie to the ransomware remains Synology's pointer ([CISA, 2026-11-18](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/alerts/2026/11/18/cisa-adds-four-known-exploited-vulnerabilities-catalog)). The four additions of that day include none of the four September flaws, so 360's naming of CVE-2026-13639 and CVE-2026-13684 stays uncorroborated. The fixed builds in Synology-SA-24:20 are unchanged: 7.2.2-72806-1, 7.2.1-69057-6, 7.2-64570-4 and, on older branches, 7.1.1-42962-7 and 6.2.4-25556-8 ([Synology PSIRT, 2024-11-05](https://www.synology.com/en-global/security/advisory/Synology_SA_24_20)).
