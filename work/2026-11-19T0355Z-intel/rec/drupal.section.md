The release the pre-announcement described is out. Drupal published SA-CORE-2026-017 (CVE-2026-132165, Critical 18 of 25) and SA-CORE-2026-018 (CVE-2026-132166, Moderately critical 14 of 25, not directly exploitable) on 2026-11-18, fixed in Drupal 11.4.13, 11.3.22 and 10.6.22 ([Drupal Security Team, 2026-11-18](https://www.drupal.org/sa-core-2026-017), [Drupal Security Team, 2026-11-18](https://www.drupal.org/sa-core-2026-018)). SA-CORE-2026-017 says an attacker can use the installer to attempt to reinstall Drupal, which can lead to a site takeover or remote code execution, and the only workaround is to secure the installer ([Drupal Security Team, 2026-11-18](https://www.drupal.org/sa-core-2026-017)). The 18 of 25 rating in the pre-announcement is SA-CORE-2026-017's; the advisories list neither a component beyond the installer nor any exploitation.
