    How to read this

    Cohort tiles count entries whose frontmatter carries the relevant taxonomy values, bucketed by the ISO week of discovered_at. Entity momentum counts entries linked to each registry entity; technique momentum counts entries mapping each ATT&CK id. Everything is post-hoc analytics over published entries; no separate data source.

    The cohorts are coarse on purpose · they mirror the deployment's trend cohorts in config/branding.yaml. For finer slicing, use the per-tag list pages under /tags/.
