    HIGHexploitedupdatedNATOA2

    Danfoss AK-SM and AK-SC, Copeland xweb and Dixell xweb refrigeration controllers compromised in France, six more European countries and Switzerland, with France blaming the IRGC unit Shahid Kaveh for the October campaign; Midnight Blue reports six unpatched pre-authentication flaws in AK-SM 800A firmware up to 5.3.2

    Coopérative U, Intermarché and E. Leclerc confirmed to AFP a cyberattack on their refrigeration systems overnight on 11 to 12 October 2026 (AFP, 2026-10-12). CERT-FR knows of numerous compromises of Danfoss AK-SM and AK-SC controllers and of Copeland xweb and Dixell xweb (CERT-FR, 2026-10-16); NCSC Switzerland rates the activity actively exploited (NCSC Switzerland, 2026-10-13). On 2026-11-09 France's foreign ministry attributed the 11 to 12 October campaign, and only that campaign, to the Iranian unit Shahid Kaveh (French foreign ministry, 2026-11-09).

    Midnight Blue reports six pre-authentication remote-code-execution flaws, stack-based buffer overflows, in the Danfoss AK-SM 800A in every firmware version up to and including 5.3.2, with no CVE identifiers yet (Midnight Blue, 2026-11-11). CCB says the research also finds a flaw in the session-control mechanism itself, so Strict mode does not eliminate the risk (CCB, 2026-11-12). BSI lists no fixed version and knows of no active exploitation (translated from German) (BSI, 2026-11-13); heise reports that BSI will not rule out vulnerabilities in firmware 5.4.0 either (heise, 2026-11-13), and Danfoss's advisories page lists no advisory on them as of 2026-11-14 (Danfoss, read 2026-11-14).

    On 2026-11-02 refrigeration failures reached retailers in at least six more countries (BleepingComputer, 2026-11-02) and the first German cases followed (heise, 2026-11-03); SRF reported on 2026-11-05 the first publicly known Swiss cases, at a butcher in Köniz and three Volg stores (SRF, 2026-11-05).

    Exposure
    A Danfoss AK-SM or AK-SC refrigeration controller, or a Copeland or Dixell xweb unit, whose management interface is reachable from the internet, legacy units above all, since Danfoss told AFP that most affected units no longer receive updates (AFP, 2026-10-12); the six new flaws are reported for the AK-SM 800A (CCB, 2026-11-12). BACS says Switzerland is affected too and that the controllers also run in butchers, restaurants, pharmacies, doctors' practices, laboratories and farms, many of whose operators do not know they are reachable from the internet (translated from German) (BACS, 2026-11-10).
    Detection
    On the controller, defrost, setpoint and account changes outside a maintenance window, and defrost duration, which Le Monde reports the attackers lengthened from one hour to eight (translated from French) (Le Monde, 2026-10-12); in firewall, provider VPN or ISP logs, inbound connections to the management interface from addresses other than the contractor's. Midnight Blue says an attacker with code execution could hide the activity from any system logs (Midnight Blue, 2026-11-11), and CSIRT Italia says to check actual temperatures and alarms with instruments independent of the supervision platform (CSIRT Italia, 2026-11-04).

    Triage: a defrost cycle is normal; BFMTV reports that installations can be programmed for two hours of defrost per 24 hours, whereas the reported attack is a continuous forced defrost at a 50-degree setpoint (translated from French) (BFMTV, 2026-10-12).

    Defender takeaway
    Take any Danfoss AK-SM or AK-SC, Copeland xweb or Dixell xweb management interface off the internet now and restore access only through a secure VPN or protected network, as CERT-FR advises (CERT-FR, 2026-10-13). For the AK-SM 800A, patching and Strict mode do not close the exposure: CCB calls updating, new passwords and Strict mode insufficient on their own (CCB, 2026-11-12), while BSI still asks operators to check patch levels and, on suspicion of compromise, to change all credentials and check the attached devices (translated from German) (BSI, 2026-11-13). The AK-SM800 and AK-SC355 have no fix (NCSC Switzerland, 2026-10-13).
