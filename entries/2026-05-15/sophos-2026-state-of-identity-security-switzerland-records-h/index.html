    Sophos State of Identity Security 2026: Swiss respondents report the highest identity-breach rate of 17 countries, at 89%

    Sophos survey: Swiss organisations reported the highest identity-breach rate of 17 countries, at 89%

    first published 2026-05-15 05:00 UTC

    Analysis

    Sophos's State of Identity Security 2026, published on 2026-05-12, surveyed 5,000 IT and cybersecurity leaders across 17 countries and 14 industries: 71% reported at least one identity-related breach in the past 12 months, Switzerland reported the highest rate at 89%, followed by Mexico at 83%, and two-thirds of ransomware victims (67%) said their ransomware incident was directly tied to their most significant identity attack (Sophos, 2026-05-12). Weak management of non-human identities such as API keys and service accounts was a root cause in 41% of successful identity breaches (Sophos, 2026-05-12). Help Net Security reports that the energy, oil and gas, utilities and federal government sectors recorded the highest breach rates (Help Net Security, 2026-05-14).

    Defender takeaway
    No decision changes on survey figures alone; the two gaps the survey measures are checkable in one's own estate: more than half of companies reviewed unusual login attempts no more than once every three months, and only 34.3% rotated and audited non-human identities weekly or more often (Help Net Security, 2026-05-14).
