    CVE-2026-109618 / CVE-2026-109619, Palo Alto Networks PAN-OS and Panorama: the PHP2Shell flaws affect every release, reaching PAN-OS through its management web interface; PAN-OS hotfixes are scheduled for 2026-10-26 to 2026-10-30

    Palo Alto: PHP2Shell affects PAN-OS and Panorama; PAN-OS hotfixes due 26 to 30 October

    first published 2026-10-22 03:41 UTCupdated 2026-10-24 03:37 UTC

    Analysis

    Palo Alto Networks' advisory of 2026-10-21 lists every release of PAN-OS 12.2, 12.1, 11.2, 11.1 and 10.2 and of Panorama as affected by CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619, with no fixed build released (Palo Alto Networks, 2026-10-21); the revision of 2026-10-23 gave estimated dates from 2026-10-26 to 2026-10-30 for hotfix builds of every PAN-OS train and noted that Cortex XCORE (SaaS) and WildFire Cloud were remediated on 2026-10-21 and 2026-10-22 (Palo Alto Networks, 2026-10-23). The advisory's timeline dates a correction of the WildFire appliances WF-500 and WF-500-B to 2026-10-23, and the table now lists them as unaffected, although the revision that morning had listed them as affected; the table no longer distinguishes Panorama, a note says the issue applies to PAN-OS software on PA-Series and VM-Series firewalls and on Panorama (virtual and M-Series), and the mitigation text covers PAN-OS and Panorama together (Palo Alto Networks, 2026-10-24). The revision of 2026-10-24 added updated threat-signature and content-version details (Palo Alto Networks, 2026-10-24). The two flaws are the unauthenticated remote-code-execution bugs in the PHP runtime that the PHP project fixed in its releases of the same day (PHP, 2026-10-21). Palo Alto says no special configuration is required to be affected, that the issue strictly affects the management web interface, which includes the HTTPS web UI and the XML API service, and that a dataplane interface is affected only where a management profile is attached to it (Palo Alto Networks, 2026-10-21). It states that the GlobalProtect portal and gateway and the captive portal are not impacted (Palo Alto Networks, 2026-10-21).

    Palo Alto describes the flaws as remote code execution but scores their impact on its own product CVSS-B 8.7 (severity high, urgency moderate), with high availability impact and none on confidentiality or integrity, against a vendor-level 9.2 with high impact on all three, and says the risk is highest where management access is allowed from external addresses on the internet; restricting management to a single jump box lowers its rating to 7.1, and it is not aware of any malicious exploitation against its products (Palo Alto Networks, 2026-10-21). Unit 42 says it has seen proof of concept of the PHP flaws (Unit 42, 2026-10-21). Until fixes ship, Palo Alto recommends restricting management access to trusted internal addresses and offers Threat Prevention coverage (Threat IDs 543004 and 97622 to 97625 and twenty further IDs from 97643 to 97678, Applications and Threats content versions 9162, 9163, 9164 and later) that applies only when management traffic is routed through a dataplane interface, the certificate for inbound management traffic has been replaced, inbound management traffic is decrypted so the firewall can inspect it, and threat prevention is enabled on that traffic (Palo Alto Networks, 2026-10-24).

    Exposure
    Every PAN-OS firewall and Panorama appliance, whatever the configuration, through its management web interface; the service is reachable from the internet where the management port, or a dataplane interface carrying a management profile, answers HTTPS or XML API requests from outside the administrator range (Palo Alto Networks, 2026-10-21).
    Detection
    In management-plane access and system logs, any request from an address outside the administrator range, though Palo Alto does not say which logs record request bodies; in threat logs, hits on the Threat IDs above where the signatures are active and the four conditions hold. Palo Alto publishes no flaw-specific indicator and reports no exploitation of its products, so no post-compromise check is defined yet (Palo Alto Networks, 2026-10-21).
    Defender takeaway
    Move management access behind a jump box or trusted internal addresses now, and remove management profiles from dataplane interfaces that face untrusted networks; Palo Alto lists no workaround beyond that restriction and the Threat Prevention coverage; its revision of 2026-10-23 schedules the first PAN-OS hotfixes for 2026-10-26 (10.2.18-h13, 11.1.16-h4 and 11.2.10-h16) and the rest by 2026-10-30, so install the hotfix of each train when it is released, while the advisory's note and mitigation text cover PAN-OS and Panorama together and its table no longer distinguishes Panorama, so apply the same management-access restriction to Panorama (Palo Alto Networks, 2026-10-24).

    Palo Alto Networks revised its advisory on 2026-10-23: the WildFire appliances WF-500 and WF-500-B are now listed as affected in all versions, and the footnotes say Cortex XCORE (SaaS) was remediated on 2026-10-21 and WildFire Cloud on 2026-10-22 (Palo Alto Networks, 2026-10-23).
