    CVE-2026-109618 / CVE-2026-109619, Palo Alto Networks PAN-OS and Panorama: the PHP2Shell flaws affect every release through the management web interface, and Palo Alto reports active exploitation against internet-exposed management interfaces; all twenty hotfixes are out

    Palo Alto reports active exploitation of PAN-OS management interfaces via PHP2Shell; all 20 hotfixes now out

    first published 2026-10-22 03:41 UTCupdated 2026-10-30 03:52 UTC

    Defender actions

    • Take the management interface of every PA-Series and VM-Series firewall and Panorama off the internet now by restricting it to a jump box or trusted internal addresses, because Palo Alto reports active exploitation against internet-exposed management interfaces, and install the hotfix for your maintenance release, which Palo Alto's table lists for every line from 10.2.7 to 12.2.4 (11.2.10-h17 adds the Brotli fix).

    Analysis

    Palo Alto Networks' advisory of 2026-10-21 lists every release of PAN-OS 12.2, 12.1, 11.2, 11.1 and 10.2 and of Panorama as affected by CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619 (Palo Alto Networks, 2026-10-21); these are the unauthenticated remote-code-execution bugs in the PHP runtime that the PHP project fixed in its releases of the same day (PHP, 2026-10-21). Palo Alto says no special configuration is required to be affected and that the issue strictly affects the management web interface, which includes the HTTPS web UI and the XML API service; a dataplane interface is affected only where a management profile is attached to it, and the GlobalProtect portal and gateway and the captive portal are not (Palo Alto Networks, 2026-10-21).

    The advisory now states that active exploitation has been observed targeting the management interface exposed to the internet (Palo Alto Networks, 2026-10-28); a copy of the page taken on 2026-10-27 at 17:10 UTC said that very limited exploitation had been observed (Palo Alto Networks, 2026-10-27), and the vendor timeline's three entries of that day are two removals of hotfix estimated dates and the 16:40 UTC change that raised severity to Critical and urgency to Highest, none of which mentions exploitation (Palo Alto Networks, 2026-10-28). The vendor score is CVSS-B 9.2, with high impact on confidentiality, integrity and availability, and 7.7 where management is reachable only from an adjacent network such as a jump box (Palo Alto Networks, 2026-10-27). The advisory names no actor, victim or post-exploitation behaviour. All twenty hotfixes are released, the last of them, 11.1.7-h12, according to the advisory timeline of 2026-10-29 23:54 UTC (Palo Alto Networks, 2026-10-30); the same hotfix covers the PHP and the Brotli flaws, except 11.2.10-h16, which has only the PHP fix, while 11.2.10-h17 has both (Palo Alto Networks, 2026-10-28). Until a build is installed, Palo Alto recommends restricting management access to a jump box or trusted internal addresses and offers Threat Prevention coverage (Threat IDs 543004 and 97622 to 97625 and further IDs from 97643) that applies only when management traffic is routed through a dataplane interface, the certificate for inbound management traffic has been replaced, inbound management traffic is decrypted and threat prevention is enabled on it (Palo Alto Networks, 2026-10-27).

    Exposure
    Every PAN-OS firewall and Panorama appliance below its train's hotfix, whatever the configuration, through its management web interface; the service is reachable from the internet where the management port, or a dataplane interface carrying a management profile, answers HTTPS or XML API requests from outside the administrator range, and that is the exposure Palo Alto says is being attacked (Palo Alto Networks, 2026-10-28).
    Detection
    In management-plane access and system logs, any request from an address outside the administrator range, though Palo Alto does not say which logs record request bodies; in threat logs, hits on the Threat IDs above where the signatures are active and the four conditions hold. Palo Alto publishes no indicator for the observed attacks and defines no post-compromise check, while CISA flags both PHP flaws in its catalog with forensic triage requirements under its directive BOD 26-04 (CISA KEV, 2026-10-27).
    Defender takeaway
    Move management access behind a jump box or trusted internal addresses now, on Panorama as on PAN-OS, and remove management profiles from dataplane interfaces that face untrusted networks; Palo Alto lists no workaround beyond that restriction and the Threat Prevention coverage. Install the hotfix for your maintenance line, which the advisory table now lists for every line (Palo Alto Networks, 2026-10-30).
