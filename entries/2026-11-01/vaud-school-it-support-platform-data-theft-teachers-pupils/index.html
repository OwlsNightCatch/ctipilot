    Canton Vaud: school IT-support platform breached, data of about 3,500 teachers and 38,000 pupils copied; pupil arrested

    first published 2026-11-01 03:35 UTCupdated 2026-11-04 03:27 UTC

    Analysis

    The Swiss canton of Vaud says unauthorised access, detected on 2026-10-26, to the IT-support platform of its Gymnases and professional schools copied the names, school e-mail addresses and support requests of about 3,500 teachers and 38,000 pupils; passwords are not stored on the platform and no grades or pupil files are affected (Etat de Vaud, 2026-10-31). The canton names targeted fraudulent messages that pose as the school or IT support as the main risk (Etat de Vaud, 2026-10-31); it gives no access method, actor or platform product. Vaud police announced on 2026-11-03 the arrest, on 2026-10-31, of a 16-year-old pupil of the affected schools, suspected of the intrusion between 23 and 26 October; he was questioned and released, his equipment was seized, and the juvenile court's investigation aims to establish how the intrusion happened and whether data were downloaded or passed to third parties (Le Temps, 2026-11-03, translated from French; Inside IT, 2026-11-03).

    Defender takeaway
    The sources give no access method, so there is no patch or configuration change to prescribe; school and cantonal IT teams should brief users to expect impersonation of IT support and to report urgent or unusual messages, as the canton advises (Etat de Vaud, 2026-10-31).

    Cited evidence

    Updates3

    Improvement03 Nov 2026 03:46 UTC

    The education directorate's deputy director said that at present much points to an internal matter, with the case and several indications passed to the police (translated from German) (Inside IT, 2026-11-02).

    Update04 Nov 2026 03:27 UTC

    Vaud's cantonal police announced on 2026-11-03 that a 16-year-old was arrested on Saturday 2026-10-31, suspected of intruding into the IT-support platform of the canton's post-compulsory schools and extracting data from it between 23 and 26 October 2026 (translated from French) (Le Temps, 2026-11-03). The education directorate says the suspect is one of its pupils and faces an administrative sanction, up to exclusion, that runs apart from the criminal case; he was questioned and released, his IT equipment was seized and is being analysed, and the investigation, led by the juvenile court, aims to establish the circumstances of the intrusion and whether data were downloaded or passed to third parties (Le Temps, 2026-11-03; Inside IT, 2026-11-03). The platform was disconnected from the internet on detection and then secured with the support of the canton's security operations centre, according to the police communiqué; the access method and the platform product are still not stated (Le Temps, 2026-11-03).

    Improvement08 Nov 2026 14:09 UTC

    The police communiqué, published on vd.ch, says the platform was disconnected from the internet as soon as the access was detected and then secured with the support of the canton of Vaud's operational security centre, that a criminal complaint was filed, and that the suspect was identified quickly and released after questioning; the cyber investigation division of the criminal police leads the inquiry under the juvenile court (translated from French) (Etat de Vaud, 2026-11-03). The education directorate's release adds that the incident was reported to the Federal Office for Cybersecurity and to the cantonal data-protection authority (translated from French) (Etat de Vaud, 2026-10-31).
