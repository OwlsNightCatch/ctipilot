    Pwn2Own Berlin 2026 results: 47 zero-days and $1,298,250 awarded, DEVCORE Master of Pwn, AI coding agents and inference tools exploited

    Pwn2Own Berlin 2026 paid $1,298,250 for 47 zero-days; DEVCORE won as Exchange, ESXi and AI coding agents fell

    first published 2026-05-17 05:00 UTC

    Analysis

    Pwn2Own Berlin 2026, ZDI's second Berlin contest after the inaugural 2025 event, ran at OffensiveCon from 14 to 16 May (Zero Day Initiative, 2026-03-12) (BleepingComputer, 2026-05-15). For 2026 ZDI split its AI category into AI Databases, Coding Agents, Local Inferences and NVIDIA (Zero Day Initiative, 2026-03-12). Targets ran fully patched in their default configurations (Zero Day Initiative, contest rules), and vendors have 90 days to patch after the zero-days are disclosed at the contest (BleepingComputer, 2026-05-15). Before it began, Hackread wrote that it had reportedly run out of space and that some researchers turned away were releasing their findings instead (Hackread, 2026-05-12).

    The final totals were $1,298,250 for 47 unique zero-days; DEVCORE won Master of Pwn with 50.5 points and $505,000, ahead of STARLabs SG (25 points, $242,500) and Out Of Bounds (12.75 points, $95,750) (Zero Day Initiative, 2026-05-16).

    Enterprise and platform targets. Orange Tsai of DEVCORE chained four logic bugs for a Microsoft Edge sandbox escape ($175,000) on Day One (Zero Day Initiative, 2026-05-14) and three bugs for remote code execution as SYSTEM on Microsoft Exchange ($200,000) on Day Two (Zero Day Initiative, 2026-05-15); neither ZDI nor BleepingComputer states whether the chain needs authentication. splitline of DEVCORE chained two bugs against Microsoft SharePoint ($100,000), and Nguyen Hoang Thach of STARLabs SG used a memory corruption bug against VMware ESXi with the cross-tenant code execution add-on ($200,000) (Zero Day Initiative, 2026-05-16), an entry that runs code from a guest on the hypervisor (Zero Day Initiative, contest rules). Windows 11 and Red Hat Enterprise Linux for Workstations fell to privilege escalations (improper access control, heap overflow, use-after-free, integer overflow and race-condition bugs), and NV Container Toolkit to chompie ($50,000) and to 0xDACA and Noam Trobishi with a use-after-free ($25,000) (Zero Day Initiative, 2026-05-14) (Zero Day Initiative, 2026-05-15) (Zero Day Initiative, 2026-05-16).

    AI targets. OpenAI Codex fell to Compass Security with a single CWE-150 bug ($40,000), while a Doyensec entry used a bug previously known to the vendor and a Viettel attempt failed (Zero Day Initiative, 2026-05-14); Summoning Team ($20,000) (Zero Day Initiative, 2026-05-15) and Satoki Tsuji of Ikotas Labs, through an external control ($20,000), also exploited it (Zero Day Initiative, 2026-05-16). Cursor fell to Viettel ($30,000) and Compass Security ($15,000) (Zero Day Initiative, 2026-05-15). LM Studio fell to STARLabs SG (five bugs) and LiteLLM to k3vg3n (three bugs), both chains including SSRF and code injection and each worth $40,000 (Zero Day Initiative, 2026-05-14); OtterSec took LM Studio with a code injection bug ($20,000) (Zero Day Initiative, 2026-05-15). Out Of Bounds took Chroma with two bugs (CWE-190, CWE-362; $20,000), and NVIDIA Megatron Bridge fell three times (Zero Day Initiative, 2026-05-14). Every Anthropic Claude Code entry, Summoning Team's Claude Desktop entry, the Ollama entry and two LiteLLM entries hit previously known bugs or collisions (Zero Day Initiative, 2026-05-14) (Zero Day Initiative, 2026-05-15) (Zero Day Initiative, 2026-05-16), and an Oracle Autonomous AI Database attempt failed (Zero Day Initiative, 2026-05-14).

    Exposure
    Current releases of the products named above, whose vendors received the bugs at the contest; ZDI's result pages give no CVE ids (Zero Day Initiative, contest rules) (Zero Day Initiative, 2026-05-16).
    Defender takeaway
    No change on the results alone: no source reports these bugs used in the wild, and vendors have 90 days to patch them (BleepingComputer, 2026-05-15).
