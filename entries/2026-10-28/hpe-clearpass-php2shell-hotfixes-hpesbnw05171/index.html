    CVE-2026-109618 / CVE-2026-109619, HPE Networking ClearPass Policy Manager: bulletin HPESBNW05171 lists the 6.14, 6.12 and 6.11 trains as affected by the PHP2Shell flaws and names a hotfix patch for each (CVSS 3.1 9.8)

    HPE ClearPass 6.14, 6.12 and 6.11 are affected by PHP2Shell; a hotfix patch exists for each train

    first published 2026-10-28 03:51 UTCupdated 2026-10-30 03:53 UTC

    Defender actions

    • Install the HPE ClearPass 6.14.1, 6.12.8 or 6.11.16 hotfix patch for CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619 on every ClearPass Policy Manager node from the HPE Networking Support Portal, and until it is installed take the guest portal and the CLI and web management interfaces off public networks, confining management to a dedicated layer 2 segment or behind firewall policy.

    Analysis

    HPE Networking's bulletin HPESBNW05171, released on 2026-10-22 and revised on 2026-10-23 and 2026-10-29, covers CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619, the PHP flaws behind PHP2Shell, in ClearPass Policy Manager and scores each CVSS 3.1 9.8 with network access, no privileges and no user interaction (HPE Networking, 2026-10-29). Its affected-products note lists ClearPass 6.14.0 and earlier in the 6.14 release train and 6.11.15 and earlier in the 6.11 train, while its machine-readable record also lists the 6.12 train and gives 6.14.1, 6.11.16 and 6.12.8 as the boundaries; the bulletin does not reconcile the two (HPE Networking, 2026-10-29). The fix is a hotfix patch for each train, 6.14.1, 6.12.8 (added in revision 3, which HPE says "exceptionally" includes 6.12.8) and 6.11.16, downloadable from the HPE Networking Support Portal; versions past end of maintenance are presumed affected and are not covered, and HPE has not assessed versions past end of support, which it says should be considered potentially impacted (HPE Networking, 2026-10-29).

    HPE recommends confining the CLI and web management interfaces to a dedicated layer 2 segment or VLAN, or controlling them with firewall policy at layer 3 and above, and asks customers to consider removing guest portals and all management interfaces from public access until the affected ClearPass instances have been upgraded (HPE Networking, 2026-10-29). Other HPE Networking products and software versions it does not list are not known to be affected, and for Mist products the bulletin points to a Juniper reference advisory (HPE Networking, 2026-10-29). CISA added both PHP flaws to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog on 2026-10-26 (CISA KEV, 2026-10-27); the bulletin itself says nothing about exploitation of ClearPass (HPE Networking, 2026-10-29).

    Exposure
    Every ClearPass Policy Manager on the 6.14, 6.12 or 6.11 train without the hotfix patch; the guest portal and the management interfaces are the surfaces HPE asks to take off public access (HPE Networking, 2026-10-29).
    Detection
    HPE gives no indicator and no post-patch check; CISA's PHP alert advises monitoring for PHP worker processes launching shells or downloading tools (CISA, 2026-10-21), which on a ClearPass node would be processes started by the web worker account (an inference, since HPE does not describe how ClearPass runs PHP).
    Defender takeaway
    Install the hotfix patch for 6.14.1, 6.12.8 or 6.11.16 on every node now, the remediation HPE's bulletin names, and take any guest portal or management interface reachable from untrusted networks off public access, as HPE asks (HPE Networking, 2026-10-29).
