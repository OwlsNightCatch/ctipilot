A regulatory follow-up worth a defender's attention because the root cause is mundane and universal. South Korea's Personal Information Protection Commission issued its largest-ever penalty against e-commerce platform Coupang, attributing a breach of tens of millions of customer records to a signing key that a former employee had stolen before departing and then used to harvest customer data undetected for months ([The Record](https://therecord.media/south-korea-data-breach-record-fine-coupang); [daily 06-13](/briefs/2026-06-13/)). Key custody and anomaly detection on signing-key use are the controls that failed — and the months of undetected access is the part that turned an insider theft into a record fine.
