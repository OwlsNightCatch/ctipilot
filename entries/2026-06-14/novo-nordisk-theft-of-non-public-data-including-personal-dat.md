Danish pharmaceutical maker Novo Nordisk disclosed on 11 June that an external party gained unauthorised access to a limited number of internal IT systems and copied non-public data, including personal data ([Novo Nordisk](https://www.novonordisk.com/news-and-media/news-and-ir-materials/news-details.html?id=916571); [daily 06-13](/briefs/2026-06-13/)). The company's statement does not itemise the data categories beyond "personal data"; pharma and life-sciences SOCs should nonetheless treat research-data and personal-data repositories as crown-jewel assets, given their value for both espionage and extortion.
