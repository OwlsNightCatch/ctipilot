The European Data Protection Board adopted a common EU/EEA personal-data-breach notification template under GDPR Article 33 at its 10 June plenary, opening it for public consultation until 5 August ([EDPB](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/news/news/2026/edpb-meets-eu-commissioner-mcgrath-and-adopts-common-data-breach-notification_en); [daily 06-11](/briefs/2026-06-11/)). **What to do differently:** breach-response runbooks that currently target per-DPA notification formats should be reviewed against the harmonised template during the consultation window — a single common format reduces the cross-border friction that has historically slowed multi-jurisdiction breach reporting, but only if your incident-response process pre-maps its fields.
