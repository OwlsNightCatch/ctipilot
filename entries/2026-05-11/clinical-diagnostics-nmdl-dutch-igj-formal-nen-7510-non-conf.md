The IGJ ruling formally found Clinical Diagnostics / NMDL non-conformant with NEN 7510 (Dutch information-security-management standard for healthcare) at the time of the July 2025 ransomware breach (approximately 941,000 patients affected per Computable / daily 2026-05-14, cervical-cancer screening data exposed). First IGJ NEN 7510 non-conformity finding against a third-party diagnostics provider. For Swiss / EU public-sector defenders: this is the regulatory template member-state regulators are likely to deploy under NIS2 essential-entity supplier-due-diligence obligations — Dutch hospitals using the same supplier and other EU member-state regulators with parallel healthcare-ISO standards (NEN 7510, ISO 27799, the Italian AgID guidelines) will pattern-match this ruling for their own supplier oversight ([IGJ inspection report](https://www.igj.nl/actueel/nieuws/2026/05/13/clinical-diagnostics-voldeed-niet-aan-wettelijke-norm-voor-informatiebeveiliging); [Computable](https://www.computable.nl/2026/05/13/inspectie-vernietigend-over-beveiliging-clinical-diagnostics-na-datahack/); [daily 2026-05-14](/briefs/2026-05-14/)).
