    XWEB EVO refrigeration controllers: two of 14 flaws exploited, unauthenticated root RCE; password flaws stay unfixed

    first published 2026-11-13 04:02 UTCupdated 2026-11-15 14:53 UTC

    Defender actions

    • Find every Dixell or Copeland XWEB EVO supervisor reachable from the internet and put it behind a VPN now; on each unit that was exposed, review its users, alarm settings and setpoints and read cabinet temperatures directly, because VulnCheck sees two of its unauthenticated flaws exploited and the password flaws will never be fixed.

    Analysis

    VulnCheck, the assigning CNA, describes unauthenticated command injection in XWEB EVO through the password field (VulnCheck, 2026-11-12) and the username field (VulnCheck, 2026-11-12) of its login and API scripts, and through the sh parameter of the setuid xwconsole.cgi (VulnCheck, 2026-11-12), all reaching root; a hard-coded password for the built-in Administrator account that gives a full session in one request (VulnCheck, 2026-11-12); and Administrator and root passwords that can be computed offline from the device MAC address and the date (VulnCheck, 2026-11-12; VulnCheck, 2026-11-12; VulnCheck, 2026-11-12). The cluster holds 14 records, and the seven scored 9.2 or higher are the ones covered here (ENISA EUVD, 2026-11-12). VulnCheck says the injection and backdoor-password flaws belong to the pre-2021 builds and are fixed on the current branch, while the predictable-password flaws reach the last firmware, 5.8.8 (VulnCheck, 2026-11-13). Copeland's bulletin says software support ended on 2026-03-16 with no further firmware or security updates (Copeland, 2026-03-16), so those will stay unfixed; its public download pages list EVO packages only up to 5.2.3 of 2018 (Copeland, 2026-11-13).

    VulnCheck's canary sensors recorded in-the-wild exploitation of the unauthenticated configuration-file write CVE-2026-124014 (CVSS 4.0 8.8, XWEB EVO 4.3.0 through 5.8.8) from 2026-11-12 22:00 UTC and four root-command probes against CVE-2026-124010 from 2026-11-13 03:41 UTC, from two opportunistic scanners it does not tie to the October refrigeration campaign (VulnCheck, 2026-11-13; VulnCheck, 2026-11-12). Copeland's bulletin of 2026-10-14, carried in the Dixell France guide that CERT-FR links, acknowledges a vulnerability on an end-of-life generation of XWEB EVO controllers and tells operators to remove all XWEB EVO units from the public internet immediately (translated from French) (Dixell France, 2026-10-14); NCSC Switzerland says that guidance addresses end-of-service XWEB EVO systems and that the public information does not confirm exploitation of the Danfoss CVEs it lists (NCSC Switzerland, 2026-11-06), and BACS lists Copeland and Dixell XWEB as potentially affected (BACS, 2026-11-10). The one compromised-unit log read concerns an XWEB Pro, not an EVO (Ransom-ISAC, 2026-11-06).

    Exposure
    An XWEB EVO unit, sold until October 2023 (Copeland, 2026-03-16), whose login page appears when the site's public address is opened from outside the site network, which is the vendor's own test (Dixell France, 2026-10-14).
    Detection
    In web or proxy access logs in front of the supervisor, an unauthenticated POST to config.lua or an unauthenticated GET to /cgi-bin/xwconsole.cgi with an sh parameter is VulnCheck's request-level signal (VulnCheck, 2026-11-13). After suspected access the vendor's procedure is to review the user list and profiles and the alarm categories, levels and recipients with a test alarm, and to read product temperatures directly in the cabinets because setpoints or alarm thresholds may have changed; CERT-FR says a factory reset removes persistence but leaves an end-of-life unit vulnerable (Dixell France, 2026-10-14; CERT-FR, 2026-10-16).
    Defender takeaway
    VulnCheck sees two flaws exploited (VulnCheck, 2026-11-13) and the password flaws will never be fixed, so keep XWEB EVO units off the internet and reach them only through a VPN, install the latest build from Copeland's update page on any pre-2021 unit, and plan the move to the supported XWEB Pro (Dixell France, 2026-10-14).

    Update15 Nov 2026 14:53 UTC

    VulnCheck's canary sensors recorded in-the-wild exploitation of CVE-2026-124014, an unauthenticated write of the system configuration file through config.lua that affects XWEB EVO 4.3.0 through 5.8.8 (CVSS 4.0 8.8), from 2026-11-12 22:00 UTC, and four probes running a root command through CVE-2026-124010 from 2026-11-13 03:41 UTC; it added both to its own exploited-vulnerabilities list and reads both sources as opportunistic scanners, not the October refrigeration campaign (VulnCheck, 2026-11-13; VulnCheck, 2026-11-12). The same disclosure says the injection and backdoor-password class was removed in the 2021 rewrite and is fixed on the current branch, so the unfixed exposure is the pre-2021 fleet plus the predictable-password flaws, which reach the last firmware (VulnCheck, 2026-11-13). For CVE-2026-124014 the two VulnCheck sources differ: the blog lists it with the pre-2021 class, while the CNA record gives XWEB EVO 4.3.0 through 5.8.8 as affected (VulnCheck, 2026-11-12). Copeland's guide tells operators to install the latest build for their model from its System Software Update page while they migrate (translated from French) (Dixell France, 2026-10-14).
