The Aargau government council adopted the information-security ordinance (InfoSiV) and decided that the information-security legislation, the law (InfoSiG) and the ordinance, enters into force on 1 January 2027 (translated from German) ([Kanton Aargau, 2026-11-06](https://www.ag.ch/de/medien/medienmitteilungen?mm=bulletin-zur-regierungsratssitzung-b790d5a8-ec12-4162-98c6-daac664c6033_de)). The canton says the law anchors uniform standards and a holistic information-security system in the cantonal administration, that an information-security specialist unit is to advise the cantonal bodies and be equipped with directive and enforcement powers so that one detection and defence strategy can be implemented, and that a further specialist unit for personnel security vetting and a cantonal cyber coordination office are being set up, both at the cantonal police (translated from German) ([Kanton Aargau, 2026-11-06](https://www.ag.ch/de/medien/medienmitteilungen?mm=bulletin-zur-regierungsratssitzung-b790d5a8-ec12-4162-98c6-daac664c6033_de)). The government elected Bernhard Droz, currently head of IT forensics and cybercrime at the cantonal police, as head of the cyber coordination office from 1 January 2027 (translated from German) ([Kanton Aargau, 2026-11-06](https://www.ag.ch/de/medien/medienmitteilungen?mm=bulletin-zur-regierungsratssitzung-b790d5a8-ec12-4162-98c6-daac664c6033_de)).

The communiqué says nothing about communes or other bearers of public tasks. In February 2025 Netzwoche reported that a majority in the consultation approved bringing them into the law's scope and that entry into force was then planned for 1 July 2026, so the final text decides whether communal bodies are bound ([Netzwoche, 2025-02-24](https://www.netzwoche.ch/news/2025-02-24/kanton-aargau-will-cybersicherheit-staerken)).

**Exposure:** Aargau cantonal bodies from 1 January 2027; whether communes and other bearers of public tasks fall in scope is not stated in the communiqué.

**Defender takeaway:** from 1 January 2027 Aargau cantonal bodies answer to one information-security specialist unit that is to be equipped to issue binding directives, so the 2026 task is reading the ordinance and the directives the specialist unit issues; the canton describes the unit's planned powers and not detailed duties, and communal IT owners should read the final text to see whether they are covered.
