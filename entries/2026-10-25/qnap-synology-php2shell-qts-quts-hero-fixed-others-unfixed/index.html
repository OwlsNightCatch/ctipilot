    PHP2Shell hits QNAP NAS before login: QTS, QuTS hero, QVR, TVR OS and Notes Station 3 fixed, QNE still unfixed

    first published 2026-10-25 14:10 UTCupdated 2026-10-31 03:39 UTC

    Defender actions

    • Update every QNAP NAS on QTS, QuTS hero or QuTScloud to the builds of 2026-10-23 (QTS 6.1.0.3656 or 5.2.11.3661, QuTS hero h6.1.0.3656, h6.0.2.3656 (h5.3.x included) or h5.2.11.3661, QuTScloud c5.2.9.3661), every QVR Surveillance (ARM32 models) to 3.0.3.1756, every TVR OS to 1.0.5 and every Notes Station 3 to 3.9.15, and take any QNAP device running QNE (ADRA) off the internet until QNAP lists a fix.

    Analysis

    QNAP's advisory QSA-26-18 says the PHP2Shell flaws CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619 (CVSS 4.0 9.2) affect the PHP used in the web components of its operating systems and several of its applications, can be triggered before any application-level authentication, and could let an unauthenticated remote attacker execute code on the device (QNAP, 2026-10-24). Its revision of 2026-10-24 lists fixed builds dated 2026-10-23 for QTS (6.1.0.3656 and 5.2.11.3661), QuTS hero (h6.1.0.3656, h6.0.2.3656, which also covers h5.3.x, and h5.2.11.3661) and QuTScloud (c5.2.9.3661), while revision V1.2 of 2026-10-28 lists Notes Station 3 3.9.14 and earlier as resolved by 3.9.15 and the table lists QVR Surveillance 3.0.3 and earlier on ARM32 models as resolved by 3.0.3.1756 and TVR OS 1.0.4 and earlier as resolved by 1.0.5 (QNAP, 2026-10-28). QNE (ADRA) 1.0.7 and earlier has its own advisory, QSA-26-19, and its fixed version is still to be announced (QNAP, 2026-10-22). QNAP's PSIRT says it has observed scanning and exploitation attempts against the flaws in the wild and blocked attempts against its own website, online store and cloud services (QNAP, 2026-10-22), and its advisory says it has received no reports of exploitation targeting QNAP products (QNAP, 2026-10-24). PHP inside Container Station containers or third-party applications is not covered by QNAP's updates (QNAP, 2026-10-24).

    Synology's advisory Synology-SA-26:14 rates its PHP 7.3 to 8.4 packages, Mail Station and Synology Calendar critical and lists fixed releases for each except the end-of-life PHP 7.3 package, which gets no fix; Synology says the PHP built into DSM is not exposed to external attack surfaces and considers the risk of exploitation very low (Synology, 2026-10-22).

    Exposure
    A QNAP NAS on QTS, QuTS hero or QuTScloud below the fixed builds, or a QNAP device running QVR Surveillance (ARM32 models) below 3.0.3.1756, TVR OS below 1.0.5, Notes Station 3 below 3.9.15 or QNE (ADRA), whose management or web ports are reachable from outside, which QNAP ties to router port forwarding, UPnP and myQNAPcloud publishing; the management ports default to 8080 and 443 (QNAP, 2026-10-24). On Synology, a DSM with one of the listed PHP packages, Mail Station or Calendar installed (Synology, 2026-10-22).
    Detection
    QNAP's compromise check is a review of QuLog Center for unexpected logins or access and a Security Center and Malware Remover scan with current definitions; if compromise is suspected it says to disconnect the device and change all passwords (QNAP, 2026-10-24).
    Defender takeaway
    Update QTS, QuTS hero, QuTScloud, Notes Station 3, QVR Surveillance (ARM32 models) and TVR OS now; for QNE (ADRA), which has no fix, remove internet exposure (port forwarding, UPnP, myQNAPcloud publishing), use a VPN for remote access and allow the management ports only from trusted subnets, which QNAP says reduces exposure without fixing the flaws and does not protect against local devices that are already compromised (QNAP, 2026-10-24). On Synology, update the listed packages; the PHP 7.3 package gets no fix (Synology, 2026-10-22).

    As of 2026-10-27 QNAP's advisory table lists QVR Surveillance 3.0.3 and earlier (ARM32 models) as resolved by QVR Surveillance 3.0.3.1756 and TVR OS 1.0.4 and earlier as resolved by TVR OS 1.0.5, while Notes Station 3 3.9.14 and earlier and QNE (ADRA) 1.0.7 and earlier remain in "Fixing" with the fixed version to be announced; QuMagie is no longer among the listed affected products, and the revision history still ends at V1.1 of 2026-10-24, so these table changes carry no dated revision (QNAP, 2026-10-27).     Update31 Oct 2026 03:39 UTC

    QNAP revised advisory QSA-26-18 to V1.2 on 2026-10-28 ("Notes Station 3 Resolved Published"), and its table now lists Notes Station 3 3.9.14 and earlier as resolved by Notes Station 3 3.9.15 (QNAP, 2026-10-28).
