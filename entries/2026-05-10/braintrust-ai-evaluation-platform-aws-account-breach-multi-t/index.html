    Braintrust AI evaluation platform AWS account breach: customer-stored AI provider API keys likely exposed, all customers urged to rotate org-level keys

    Braintrust AWS account breach likely exposed customers' stored AI provider keys; every customer asked to rotate them

    first published 2026-05-10 05:00 UTC

    Analysis

    Braintrust, an AI evaluation and observability platform, discovered on 2026-05-04, after a report of suspicious behaviour, that attackers had accessed one of its AWS accounts (SecurityWeek, 2026-05-08). It emailed customers on Monday 2026-05-04 and disclosed the incident on its website on Tuesday 2026-05-05 (TechCrunch, 2026-05-06); SecurityWeek dates the customer email to 2026-05-05 (SecurityWeek, 2026-05-08). The internal AWS account likely gave the attackers access to the org-level API keys customers store with Braintrust to access AI models; at least one customer was affected and three others reported suspicious spikes in AI provider usage, while Braintrust said it had not identified broader customer exposure (SecurityWeek, 2026-05-08). Braintrust locked down the compromised account, audited and restricted access across related systems and rotated internal secrets; the cause of the breach is under investigation (TechCrunch, 2026-05-06). Nudge Security CTO Jaime Blasco told SecurityWeek the exposed keys were likely stored for AI-forward companies such as Box, Cloudflare, Dropbox, Notion, Ramp and Stripe, and called every AI evaluation, observability and gateway tool a company adopts a credential warehouse (SecurityWeek, 2026-05-08).

    Exposure
    Organisations that stored org-level AI provider keys in Braintrust; Braintrust informed all org admins with stored AI provider secrets (SecurityWeek, 2026-05-08).
    Detection
    AI provider usage and billing telemetry for the keys that were stored in Braintrust: the signal three customers reported was a suspicious spike in AI provider usage (SecurityWeek, 2026-05-08).
    Defender takeaway
    Braintrust customers delete or revoke the existing AI provider secrets on the org-level settings page, configure new ones and confirm the rotation from their timestamps, as Braintrust recommends (SecurityWeek, 2026-05-08), and review the provider-side usage of the old keys up to their revocation; organisations that stored no keys in Braintrust have no decision to make.
